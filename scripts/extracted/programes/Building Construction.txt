Dr Isa El-Buba Foundation (DISEF)
Departmental Training Module
Building Construction

1. Department Overview
The Building Construction Department trains young people to understand the basic principles, stages, materials, tools, safety practices and work discipline required in building construction.
Building construction is the process of creating houses, classrooms, offices, shops, worship centres, hospitals, hostels and other structures that serve human needs. It includes site preparation, setting-out, foundation work, block work, concrete work, roofing awareness, plastering, flooring, painting, services awareness, maintenance and finishing.
This department does not aim to turn trainees into fully qualified builders, engineers or architects within seven months. Rather, it gives trainees a strong practical foundation so they can work safely under supervision, assist skilled workers, understand building sites, keep simple records, care for tools and materials, and prepare for industrial attachment.
Building Construction is a serious department because buildings affect human life. A poorly built structure can waste money, cause injury or even lead to loss of life. A well-trained trainee must therefore learn accuracy, patience, safety, teamwork, honesty and excellence.

2. Purpose of the Department
The purpose of the Building Construction Department is to raise disciplined, skilled and God-fearing trainees who can participate responsibly in building work under supervision and grow towards useful employment or enterprise.
The department aims to:
1. Teach trainees the dignity and importance of building work.
2. Introduce trainees to the stages of building construction.
3. Train trainees in site discipline, safety and tool handling.
4. Teach basic measurement, setting-out and foundation awareness.
5. Introduce block work, mortar, concrete, formwork and finishing.
6. Build accuracy, patience, orderliness and teamwork.
7. Prepare trainees for industrial attachment on building sites and construction-related workplaces.
8. Help trainees identify honest enterprise opportunities in building services.
9. Develop trainees who can serve communities through safe and quality building work.

8. Safety Rules
Building construction work can be dangerous when tools, materials, trenches, heights, electricity, dust and heavy objects are handled carelessly.
General Building Site Safety Rules
1. Do not enter a building site without permission.
2. Wear required protective equipment.
3. Do not run on site.
4. Do not throw tools or materials.
5. Do not stand under unsafe or suspended materials.
6. Do not touch exposed wires.
7. Do not operate machines without training and permission.
8. Do not enter trenches without supervisor approval.
9. Do not climb unsafe ladders or scaffolds.
10. Keep pathways clear.
11. Carry blocks and materials carefully.
12. Report injuries immediately.
13. Report unsafe tools.
14. Keep sharp objects away from walkways.
15. Store materials properly.
16. Wash hands after handling cement.
17. Avoid inhaling dust where possible.
18. Do not joke or fight during practical work.
19. Keep the work area clean.
20. Obey trainer and supervisor instructions at all times.
Cement and Concrete Safety
1. Avoid prolonged skin contact with cement.
2. Wear gloves where available.
3. Wash cement from skin quickly.
4. Do not allow cement dust into eyes.
5. Use masks where dust is heavy.
6. Keep cement dry.
7. Do not mix concrete without supervision.
8. Clean tools after use.
Height and Roofing Safety
1. Do not climb roofs without permission.
2. Do not use ladders without supervision.
3. Do not stand on weak roofing sheets.
4. Do not work at height without proper safety guidance.
5. Report unsafe ladders or platforms.
6. Keep tools secure when working above ground.
Electrical Safety Awareness
1. Do not touch exposed wires.
2. Do not repair electrical faults unless trained and authorised.
3. Report electrical faults immediately.
4. Keep water away from electrical points.
5. Do not overload sockets or extension cables.
6. Treat all electrical hazards as dangerous.
Safety Scripture
Proverbs 22:3“A prudent man foreseeth the evil, and hideth himself: but the simple pass on, and are punished.”
Safety Character Lesson
Safety is wisdom. A disciplined building trainee protects life, tools, materials and the reputation of the Academy.
