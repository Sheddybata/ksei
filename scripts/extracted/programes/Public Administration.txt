Departmental Training Module
Public Administration
1. Department Overview
The Public Administration Department trains young people to understand the basic principles, discipline, ethics, systems and practical skills required for serving in government, public institutions, community organisations, ministries, agencies, local councils, public offices and development programmes.
Public administration is the organised way through which policies, laws, services, programmes and public resources are managed for the benefit of society. It involves planning, records, public service delivery, communication, budgeting awareness, accountability, community engagement, policy implementation, supervision, public ethics and responsible leadership.
A nation cannot grow properly without good public administration. Roads, schools, hospitals, water projects, markets, public records, local government services, community development, public safety and social welfare all require administration. When public administration is weak, people suffer from delay, corruption, poor records, abandoned projects, injustice, waste and lack of trust.
This department does not aim to turn trainees into senior civil servants within seven months. Rather, it gives them strong foundational knowledge in public service, governance, policy awareness, office procedure, citizen service, public records, public accountability, community engagement, teamwork, ethics and administrative responsibility.
A public administrator must be honest, disciplined, fair, respectful, patient, accountable, organised and service-minded. Public administration is not an opportunity for selfish gain. It is a platform for serving people, strengthening communities and honouring God through justice, order and faithfulness.
2. Purpose of the Department
The purpose of the Public Administration Department is to raise disciplined, ethical and God-fearing trainees who can understand public service, support administrative systems and contribute to community and national development with integrity.
The department aims to:
1. Teach trainees the meaning and importance of public administration.
2. Help trainees understand government, governance and public service.
3. Train trainees in public office conduct, records, communication and citizen service.
4. Teach policy implementation awareness and programme support.
5. Train trainees in community engagement, public accountability and service delivery.
6. Build understanding of public finance, budgeting, procurement awareness and resource stewardship.
7. Develop honesty, fairness, patience, diligence, confidentiality and respect for authority.
8. Prepare trainees for industrial attachment in local government offices, ministries, agencies, NGOs, community development offices, public institutions, schools, hospitals and administrative departments.
9. Help trainees identify enterprise and employability opportunities connected to public administration support.
10. Develop trainees who can serve society with justice, order, compassion and Kingdom character.

8. Safety Rules
Public Administration has serious ethical, legal, information, public trust, financial and reputational risks. Trainees must handle citizens, records, public resources and official communication carefully.
General Public Administration Safety and Conduct Rules
1. Do not access confidential files without permission.
2. Do not disclose citizen information.
3. Do not falsify public records.
4. Do not accept bribes.
5. Do not demand unofficial payments.
6. Do not favour friends or relatives unfairly.
7. Do not misuse public property.
8. Do not destroy documents without approval.
9. Do not sign documents without authority.
10. Do not promise services you cannot deliver.
11. Do not speak rudely to citizens.
12. Do not argue with the public.
13. Do not ignore official procedures.
14. Do not hide complaints.
15. Do not hide mistakes.
16. Do not share passwords.
17. Do not use public office resources for selfish gain.
18. Report missing files immediately.
19. Report corruption or unsafe practices through proper channels.
20. Obey trainer and supervisor instructions at all times.
Confidentiality and Data Protection Rules
1. Keep citizen records private.
2. Keep staff records private.
3. Keep financial records secure.
4. Do not leave files exposed.
5. Do not discuss confidential matters outside proper channels.
6. Do not post official information online without approval.
7. Do not photograph official documents without permission.
8. Lock files where required.
9. Use sample data for training.
10. Report data mistakes immediately.
Public Interaction Rules
1. Treat all citizens with respect.
2. Listen before responding.
3. Do not insult or mock anyone.
4. Explain procedures clearly.
5. Refer difficult matters to supervisors.
6. Protect vulnerable people.
7. Avoid discrimination.
8. Avoid political arguments in service settings.
9. Keep waiting areas orderly.
10. Report disorder or threats immediately.
Public Finance and Resource Rules
1. Do not handle public funds without authorisation.
2. Keep receipts and vouchers.
3. Record all expenses.
4. Do not borrow public money.
5. Do not divert public materials.
6. Do not inflate prices.
7. Do not hide unused materials.
8. Do not accept secret commissions.
9. Report shortages early.
10. Protect public resources.
Office and Digital Safety Rules
1. Do not overload electrical sockets.
2. Keep cables arranged safely.
3. Do not touch electrical equipment with wet hands.
4. Do not delete files without approval.
5. Save files with proper names.
6. Back up important documents where required.
7. Do not open suspicious email attachments.
8. Log out after using shared computers.
9. Protect passwords.
10. Report equipment faults early.
Safety Scripture
Proverbs 22:3“A prudent man foreseeth the evil, and hideth himself: but the simple pass on, and are punished.”
Safety Character Lesson
Public administration safety includes protecting people, records, public resources, public trust and the reputation of the Academy.

17. Departmental Closing Charge
Public Administration is a sacred and practical field. Through good public administration, citizens are served, records are protected, programmes are implemented, public resources are managed, communities are developed and justice is strengthened.
A public administrator must not be corrupt, careless, rude, biased or lazy. Public service requires patience, humility, fairness, confidentiality, order and the fear of God. The people suffer when public servants are wicked, but communities rejoice when righteous people serve with integrity.
The Academy is raising trainees who will not abuse authority, demand bribes, falsify records or treat citizens with contempt. They must become men and women who serve the public with justice, mercy, humility and excellence.
