Departmental Training Module
Welding and Fabrication
1. Department Overview
The Welding and Fabrication Department trains young people to understand the basic principles, tools, safety rules, discipline and practical skills required in joining, cutting, shaping and assembling metal materials.
Welding and fabrication are important in construction, agriculture, transport, building maintenance, road work, equipment repair, gates, doors, windows, railings, tanks, frames, metal furniture, machine support and community infrastructure.
This department does not aim to make trainees fully qualified professional welders within seven months. Welding is a high-risk skill that involves electricity, heat, fire, sparks, fumes, sharp metal, heavy materials and powerful machines. Therefore, every trainee must work under strict supervision and must not weld, grind, cut or handle gas equipment without trainer approval.
The purpose of this department is to give trainees a strong foundation in workshop safety, tool identification, metal measurement, cutting awareness, welding awareness, joint preparation, fabrication planning, finishing, repair awareness, record keeping and enterprise development.
A welding trainee must be disciplined, patient, careful, accurate and safety-conscious. A careless welder can cause burns, eye injuries, electric shock, fire, weak joints, damaged property and serious accidents. Therefore, trainees must learn to work with wisdom, humility, safety and excellence.
2. Purpose of the Department
The purpose of the Welding and Fabrication Department is to raise disciplined, skilled and God-fearing trainees who can assist in welding and fabrication work under supervision and grow towards employment, apprenticeship or enterprise.
The department aims to:
1. Teach trainees the dignity and value of welding and fabrication.
2. Introduce trainees to welding tools, machines, materials and workshop procedures.
3. Train trainees in welding workshop safety and fire prevention.
4. Teach basic measurement, marking, cutting, fitting and joint preparation.
5. Introduce trainees to arc welding, gas welding awareness, MIG/TIG awareness and grinding safety.
6. Train trainees in simple fabrication planning and supervised practice.
7. Build accuracy, patience, discipline, teamwork, honesty and tool care.
8. Prepare trainees for industrial attachment in welding workshops, fabrication yards, construction sites, equipment workshops and metal works companies.
9. Help trainees identify honest enterprise opportunities in welding and fabrication.
10. Develop trainees who can serve communities through safe, strong and useful metal work.

8. Safety Rules
Welding and fabrication work is high risk. Safety must be repeated daily. Trainees must understand that one careless action can lead to burns, blindness, electric shock, fire or serious injury.
General Workshop Safety Rules
1. Do not weld without trainer approval.
2. Do not grind without trainer approval.
3. Do not cut metal without supervision.
4. Wear proper protective equipment.
5. Do not look at a welding arc without a welding helmet.
6. Do not allow others to watch welding without eye protection.
7. Keep flammable materials away from sparks.
8. Keep fire extinguisher nearby.
9. Do not touch hot metal with bare hands.
10. Mark hot metal where necessary.
11. Keep the workshop clean.
12. Keep cables arranged safely.
13. Do not use damaged cables.
14. Do not weld in wet conditions.
15. Do not wear loose clothing around machines.
16. Do not play or fight in the workshop.
17. Report burns and injuries immediately.
18. Report damaged tools and equipment.
19. Clean the work area after use.
20. Obey trainer and supervisor instructions at all times.
Electrical Welding Safety Rules
1. Check cables before welding.
2. Do not use exposed or damaged cables.
3. Keep hands dry when handling electrical equipment.
4. Ensure proper earth connection.
5. Do not touch electrode and workpiece at the same time with bare hands.
6. Switch off machine after use.
7. Do not repair welding machines unless qualified.
8. Keep water away from electrical equipment.
9. Do not overload sockets.
10. Report electric shock immediately.
Eye and Face Safety Rules
1. Always use welding helmet during welding.
2. Use goggles during grinding.
3. Use face shield when required.
4. Do not look directly at welding arc.
5. Warn others before welding.
6. Use welding screens where available.
7. Report eye pain after welding exposure.
8. Do not remove eye protection during active grinding or cutting.
Fire Safety Rules
1. Remove flammable materials before welding.
2. Keep fire extinguisher accessible.
3. Do not weld near fuel, paint, gas or cloth.
4. Watch for sparks after welding.
5. Allow hot metal to cool safely.
6. Do not leave the workshop immediately after hot work without checking for fire.
7. Keep oily rags away from sparks.
8. Store gas cylinders safely.
9. Do not smoke in the welding area.
10. Report smoke, fire or burning smell immediately.
Grinding and Cutting Safety Rules
1. Use correct disc for the task.
2. Do not use cracked discs.
3. Ensure grinder guard is in place.
4. Hold grinder firmly.
5. Keep sparks away from people and flammable materials.
6. Wear goggles or face shield.
7. Allow grinder to stop before placing it down.
8. Do not force the grinder.
9. Keep hands away from cutting disc.
10. Disconnect grinder before changing disc.
Gas Cylinder Safety Awareness Rules
1. Gas cylinders must be handled only by trained authorised persons.
2. Keep cylinders upright and secured.
3. Do not drop or roll cylinders carelessly.
4. Keep cylinders away from heat and sparks.
5. Check hoses for damage.
6. Use flashback arrestors where required.
7. Do not use oil or grease on oxygen fittings.
8. Close cylinder valves after use.
9. Store oxygen and fuel gas safely.
10. Report leaks immediately.
Safety Scripture
Proverbs 22:3“A prudent man foreseeth the evil, and hideth himself: but the simple pass on, and are punished.”
Safety Character Lesson
Safety is wisdom. A disciplined welding trainee protects eyes, hands, life, property, tools and the reputation of the Academy.
