Dr Isa El-Buba Foundation (DISEF)
Departmental Training Module
Nation Building
1. Department Overview
The Nation Building Department trains young people to understand their responsibility to God, their community and their nation. It teaches trainees that nations are not built by government alone. Nations are built by righteous citizens, responsible families, honest leaders, disciplined workers, faithful communities, productive institutions and people who choose to serve rather than destroy.
Nation building is the process of developing a nation spiritually, morally, socially, economically, politically and culturally. It includes building unity, peace, justice, good leadership, strong institutions, public service, productivity, civic responsibility, ethical conduct, community development and national vision.
A nation becomes weak when citizens are selfish, corrupt, divided, lazy, violent, tribalistic, careless, dishonest and irresponsible. A nation becomes strong when citizens are righteous, diligent, truthful, peaceful, patriotic, productive, disciplined, just and willing to serve the common good.
This department does not aim to turn trainees into political experts within seven months. Rather, it gives them a strong foundation in civic responsibility, national values, leadership ethics, peacebuilding, community service, public accountability, unity, problem-solving, social responsibility and practical contribution to national development.
Nation building begins with personal transformation. A trainee who cannot keep time, respect authority, speak truth, reject corruption, work diligently and serve others cannot truly build a nation. Therefore, the Nation Building Department trains trainees to become citizens who love God, honour people, serve communities, respect lawful authority, reject corruption and contribute positively wherever they are placed.
2. Purpose of the Department
The purpose of the Nation Building Department is to raise God-fearing, responsible and disciplined trainees who understand their role in building a righteous, peaceful, productive and united nation.
The department aims to:
1. Teach trainees the meaning and importance of nation building.
2. Build love for God, community and country.
3. Teach civic responsibility, patriotism and lawful conduct.
4. Develop understanding of righteousness, justice, mercy and public responsibility.
5. Train trainees in peacebuilding, unity, conflict prevention and community harmony.
6. Teach trainees to reject corruption, tribal hatred, religious intolerance, violence and destructive speech.
7. Build leadership character, service mentality and community development awareness.
8. Train trainees to identify local problems and design practical solutions.
9. Prepare trainees for industrial attachment, community service and leadership responsibilities.
10. Help trainees identify opportunities to serve the nation through enterprise, public service, education, ministry, agriculture, skills and social impact.
11. Develop trainees who can become salt and light in their families, workplaces, communities and nation.

8. Safety Rules
Nation Building training involves public discussion, community service, leadership, civic responsibility, social issues and group activities. These areas must be handled with wisdom, peace and discipline.
General Nation Building Safety Rules
1. Do not promote violence.
2. Do not promote tribal hatred.
3. Do not promote religious hatred.
4. Do not insult any community or group.
5. Do not spread rumours.
6. Do not share fake news.
7. Do not use hate speech.
8. Do not engage in partisan campaigning during Academy training.
9. Do not threaten others.
10. Do not mock people’s background, language or culture.
11. Do not destroy public property.
12. Do not misuse community resources.
13. Do not speak disrespectfully to lawful authority.
14. Do not organise public activities without approval.
15. Do not lead trainees into unsafe locations.
16. Do not confront dangerous persons.
17. Report threats or risks early.
18. Use peaceful communication at all times.
19. Follow trainer and supervisor instructions.
20. Protect the reputation of the Academy.
Community Service Safety Rules
1. Do not begin any community activity without approval.
2. Conduct risk assessment before service activities.
3. Wear protective materials where needed.
4. Avoid unsafe roads or environments.
5. Work in teams.
6. Keep attendance records.
7. Report injuries immediately.
8. Respect community leaders.
9. Do not take photographs of people without permission.
10. Leave the service area orderly.
Public Speech and Social Media Safety Rules
1. Check information before sharing.
2. Avoid inflammatory language.
3. Do not attack personalities.
4. Speak with respect.
5. Do not forward unverified claims.
6. Do not post content that can incite violence.
7. Do not reveal private information.
8. Do not use the Academy’s name without approval.
9. Promote peace and responsibility.
10. Report harmful content to the trainer.
Anti-Corruption and Integrity Rules
1. Do not accept bribes.
2. Do not falsify records.
3. Do not steal project materials.
4. Do not misuse public or community resources.
5. Do not inflate costs dishonestly.
6. Do not favour friends unfairly.
7. Do not hide mistakes.
8. Do not submit false reports.
9. Keep receipts and records.
10. Report wrongdoing through proper channels.
