Departmental Training Module
Heavy Equipment Operations

1. Department Overview
The Heavy Equipment Operations Department trains young people to understand the basic principles, safety requirements, discipline and practical awareness needed for working around and operating heavy construction equipment.
Heavy equipment includes machines used for construction, road work, earthmoving, agriculture, mining support, building projects, drainage work and site preparation. Examples include excavators, bulldozers, graders, wheel loaders, backhoe loaders, rollers, compactors, tipper trucks, cranes, forklifts and tractors.
This department does not aim to turn trainees into fully licensed machine operators within seven months. Heavy equipment is powerful and dangerous. No trainee should operate any machine without proper approval, certified supervision and safety control. The purpose of this department is to give trainees strong foundational knowledge, safety discipline, machine awareness, pre-operation inspection skills, site communication skills, logbook habits and supervised practical exposure.
A heavy equipment operator must be alert, disciplined, calm, teachable, safety-conscious and responsible. A careless operator can destroy property, waste fuel, damage equipment, injure workers or cause death. Therefore, this department must train trainees to respect machines, obey supervisors, observe safety rules and work with excellence.

2. Purpose of the Department
The purpose of the Heavy Equipment Operations Department is to raise disciplined, safety-conscious and God-fearing trainees who can work responsibly around heavy machines, assist operators, understand basic machine functions and prepare for further supervised training or industrial attachment.
The department aims to:
1. Teach trainees the importance of heavy equipment in construction, roads, agriculture and community development.
2. Introduce trainees to common heavy machines and their uses.
3. Train trainees in machine safety, site safety and operator discipline.
4. Teach pre-start inspection awareness and daily machine checks.
5. Build understanding of earthmoving, loading, grading, compaction and material handling.
6. Train trainees in communication, hand signals, banksman awareness and teamwork.
7. Teach fuel discipline, machine care, logbook keeping and reporting.
8. Prepare trainees for industrial attachment with construction companies, road contractors, equipment yards, farms, public works departments and machine workshops.
9. Help trainees identify enterprise opportunities connected to heavy equipment support services.
10. Build character lessons of responsibility, diligence, caution, obedience, patience and stewardship.

5. Weekly Topics for Seven Months

8. Safety Rules
Heavy equipment work is high risk. Safety must be repeated daily. Trainees must understand that one careless action around machines can lead to serious injury or death.
General Heavy Equipment Safety Rules
1. Do not operate any machine without authorisation.
2. Do not enter a machine cabin without permission.
3. Do not touch machine controls without supervision.
4. Wear required protective equipment.
5. Keep away from machine blind spots.
6. Never stand behind reversing equipment.
7. Never stand under a suspended load.
8. Keep safe distance from moving machines.
9. Obey the banksman or signal person.
10. Do not distract operators.
11. Do not use phones near active machine work.
12. Do not run across machine paths.
13. Do not climb on machines without permission.
14. Do not refuel near fire or smoking.
15. Report leaks, damage and strange sounds.
16. Report unsafe behaviour immediately.
17. Keep pedestrians away from machine zones.
18. Use cones and signs where available.
19. Follow supervisor instructions at all times.
20. Stop work and report danger when unsure.
Machine Start-Up and Shutdown Awareness Rules
1. Only authorised operators may start machines.
2. Conduct walk-around inspection before operation.
3. Check for people around the machine before movement.
4. Use horn or warning signal where required.
5. Fasten seat belt where provided.
6. Do not start a machine with people nearby.
7. Park on stable ground.
8. Lower buckets, blades or attachments before shutdown.
9. Apply parking brake where required.
10. Remove keys only according to site procedure.
Excavation and Trench Safety Rules
1. Do not stand near unsupported trench edges.
2. Do not enter trenches without approval.
3. Keep spoil away from trench edges.
4. Watch for underground services.
5. Keep equipment away from unstable edges.
6. Report cracks, collapse or water inside trenches.
7. Keep unauthorised persons away.
Loading and Dumping Safety Rules
1. Keep clear of loading areas.
2. Stand only in approved safe zones.
3. Do not stand beside tipper bodies during dumping.
4. Watch for falling materials.
5. Follow traffic routes.
6. Do not overload equipment.
7. Maintain communication with the operator.
8. Report unstable ground.
Lifting Safety Rules
1. Do not stand under lifted loads.
2. Keep away from crane swing areas.
3. Use exclusion zones.
4. Do not touch suspended loads without instruction.
5. Report damaged lifting accessories.
6. Follow signal person instructions.
7. Do not distract lifting operators.
Safety Scripture
Proverbs 22:3“A prudent man foreseeth the evil, and hideth himself: but the simple pass on, and are punished.”
Safety Character Lesson
Safety is wisdom. A disciplined trainee protects life, equipment, property and the reputation of the Academy.
