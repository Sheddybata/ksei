Dr Isa El-Buba Foundation (DISEF)
Departmental Training Module
Road Construction

1. Department Overview
The Road Construction Department trains young people to understand the basic principles, practices and discipline of road construction and road maintenance. The department introduces trainees to road types, site safety, measurement, setting-out awareness, earthworks, drainage, compaction, road materials, pavement layers, concrete and asphalt awareness, road repairs, traffic control, quality checks, record keeping and road construction enterprise.
Roads are important for community development. Good roads help people travel, move farm produce, access hospitals, attend school, conduct business and connect villages, towns and cities. Poor roads can increase accidents, delay trade, damage vehicles and make life difficult for communities.
This department does not aim to make trainees fully qualified road engineers within seven months. Rather, it gives them a strong foundation so they can work safely under supervision, understand road construction sites, assist skilled workers, continue learning and prepare for industrial attachment in road construction companies, public works departments, construction firms and maintenance units.
Road construction requires discipline, accuracy, teamwork, safety, patience, strength and honesty. A careless road worker can waste materials, create unsafe roads and endanger lives. A faithful trainee must therefore learn to build roads with wisdom, care and integrity.

2. Purpose of the Department
The purpose of the Road Construction Department is to raise disciplined, skilled and God-fearing trainees who can participate responsibly in road construction and maintenance work under supervision.
The department aims to:
1. Teach trainees the value of roads in community and national development.
2. Introduce trainees to basic road construction materials, tools and equipment.
3. Train trainees in road site safety, measurement and setting-out awareness.
4. Teach basic road layers, drainage, earthworks and compaction principles.
5. Build discipline, accuracy, teamwork and respect for site rules.
6. Prepare trainees for industrial attachment in road construction and public works environments.
7. Help trainees understand road maintenance and small enterprise opportunities.
8. Develop trainees who can serve communities through safe, honest and quality road work.

8. Safety Rules
Road construction work can be dangerous when tools, heavy equipment, traffic, hot materials, trenches, dust and moving vehicles are handled carelessly.
General Road Site Safety Rules
1. Do not enter a road construction area without permission.
2. Wear required protective equipment.
3. Wear reflective clothing where vehicles are moving.
4. Do not run on site.
5. Do not stand behind moving vehicles or equipment.
6. Do not operate machinery without training and permission.
7. Do not touch hot asphalt or bitumen.
8. Do not enter deep drains or trenches without supervisor approval.
9. Do not stand too close to excavation edges.
10. Keep tools away from traffic paths.
11. Use warning signs and cones where available.
12. Report injuries immediately.
13. Report unsafe tools.
14. Report blocked drains, open holes and unstable ground.
15. Do not joke or fight during practical work.
16. Drink clean water and avoid unsafe water.
17. Wash hands after handling cement, soil or chemicals.
18. Keep the work area clean.
19. Obey trainer and supervisor instructions.
20. Respect road users and community members.
Traffic Safety Rules
1. Always face oncoming traffic when necessary and safe.
2. Do not cross active roads carelessly.
3. Use designated safe crossing points.
4. Place warning signs before work areas where required.
5. Do not argue with drivers.
6. Follow the supervisor’s traffic control instructions.
7. Do not stand in blind spots of vehicles.
8. Use reflective jackets where available.
9. Keep pedestrians away from unsafe work areas.
10. Report dangerous driving or unsafe traffic situations.
Tool and Material Safety
1. Use the right tool for the right task.
2. Carry sharp tools safely.
3. Do not throw tools.
4. Check tools before use.
5. Clean tools after work.
6. Return tools to storage.
7. Report lost or damaged tools.
8. Carry materials carefully.
9. Lift heavy materials with help.
10. Avoid wasting materials.
Asphalt and Concrete Safety Awareness
1. Do not touch hot asphalt.
2. Do not inhale heavy fumes where possible.
3. Do not work near hot materials without supervision.
4. Wear gloves and boots when instructed.
5. Avoid cement contact with skin.
6. Wash cement from skin quickly.
7. Do not mix concrete without supervision.
8. Clean tools after use.
Safety Scripture
Proverbs 22:3“A prudent man foreseeth the evil, and hideth himself: but the simple pass on, and are punished.”
Safety Character Lesson
Safety is wisdom. A disciplined road trainee protects life, tools, materials, road users and the reputation of the Academy.

9. Workshop Demonstrations
The trainer should demonstrate before trainees practise. Demonstrations must be safe, simple and supervised.
Key Demonstrations
1. How to identify road construction tools.
2. How to wear personal protective equipment.
3. How to move safely around a road work area.
4. How to carry tools safely.
5. How to clean and store tools.
6. How to identify road materials.
7. How to measure road width and length.
8. How to mark road edges with pegs and string.
9. How to identify slope and water flow.
10. How to clear a small work area.
11. How to inspect drains.
12. How to clear light drainage blockage safely.
13. How to spread soil or gravel.
14. How to compact a small practice area manually.
15. How to identify pavement layers.
16. How to identify potholes and cracks.
17. How to complete a road defect observation sheet.
18. How to prepare a simple road work record.
19. How to prepare a material list.
20. How to set up a simple traffic safety model.
21. How to complete a road construction attachment logbook.
22. How to prepare a road maintenance report.
Demonstration Method
Each demonstration should follow this format:
1. Explain the purpose of the task.
2. Show the tools and materials.
3. State safety rules.
4. Demonstrate slowly.
5. Explain each step.
6. Allow trainees to ask questions.
7. Let trainees practise under supervision.
8. Correct errors immediately.
9. Check quality.
10. Record lessons in the road site logbook.
Demonstration Scripture
Proverbs 4:7“Wisdom is the principal thing; therefore get wisdom: and with all thy getting get understanding.”
Demonstration Character Lesson
Trainees should not only copy actions. They must understand why each step matters.

Road construction sites can be dangerous.
Scripture
Proverbs 22:3“A prudent man foreseeth the evil, and hideth himself: but the simple pass on, and are punished.”

A wise trainee avoids danger and follows safety rules.
