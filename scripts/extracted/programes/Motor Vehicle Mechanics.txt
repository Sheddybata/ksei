Departmental Training Module
Motor Vehicle Mechanics
1. Department Overview
The Motor Vehicle Mechanics Department trains young people to understand the basic principles, tools, safety rules, service procedures and work discipline required in motor vehicle maintenance and repair.
Motor vehicles are important for transport, business, agriculture, emergency response, family movement, ministry work and community development. A well-maintained vehicle can save time, protect lives and support productivity. A poorly maintained vehicle can break down, waste money, cause accidents and endanger people.
This department introduces trainees to vehicle systems such as the engine, lubrication system, cooling system, fuel system, battery, electrical system, ignition awareness, tyres, brakes, suspension, steering, transmission awareness and basic servicing.
The department does not aim to make trainees fully qualified mechanics within seven months. Rather, it gives them strong foundational knowledge, workshop discipline, safety habits, tool handling skills, inspection awareness, basic servicing practice and readiness for industrial attachment in mechanic workshops, fleet maintenance units, transport companies, garages and vehicle service centres.
Motor Vehicle Mechanics requires patience, careful observation, honesty, cleanliness, accuracy, tool care, problem-solving and safety. A careless mechanic can damage vehicles, waste parts, cause accidents and destroy customer trust. Therefore, this department must train trainees to work with discipline, integrity and excellence.
2. Purpose of the Department
The purpose of the Motor Vehicle Mechanics Department is to raise disciplined, safety-conscious and God-fearing trainees who can assist in vehicle maintenance and repair under supervision and grow towards employment, apprenticeship or enterprise.
The department aims to:
1. Teach trainees the dignity and value of vehicle maintenance work.
2. Introduce trainees to major vehicle systems and their functions.
3. Train trainees in workshop safety, tool handling and cleanliness.
4. Teach basic vehicle inspection and fault observation.
5. Train trainees in basic servicing awareness, including oil, coolant, battery and tyre checks.
6. Build discipline, patience, accuracy, honesty and customer care.
7. Prepare trainees for industrial attachment in mechanic workshops and vehicle service environments.
8. Help trainees identify honest enterprise opportunities in motor vehicle maintenance.
9. Develop trainees who can serve communities through safe and reliable vehicle support services.

8. Safety Rules
Motor vehicle workshop work can be dangerous when fuel, batteries, hot engines, lifted vehicles, sharp tools, rotating parts and electrical systems are handled carelessly.
General Workshop Safety Rules
1. Do not work on a vehicle without permission.
2. Do not start any vehicle without trainer approval.
3. Do not test-drive any vehicle.
4. Do not work under a vehicle supported only by a jack.
5. Use axle stands when working under a lifted vehicle.
6. Use wheel chocks where needed.
7. Keep hands away from moving belts and fans.
8. Do not touch hot engine parts.
9. Do not open a hot radiator cap.
10. Do not smoke near fuel or batteries.
11. Keep fuel away from fire.
12. Clean oil spills immediately.
13. Keep tools away from walkways.
14. Wear protective clothing.
15. Report injuries immediately.
16. Report damaged tools.
17. Do not play or fight in the workshop.
18. Keep the workshop clean.
19. Protect customer property.
20. Obey trainer and supervisor instructions.
Battery Safety Rules
1. Do not short battery terminals.
2. Do not place metal tools across battery terminals.
3. Wear eye protection where available.
4. Avoid battery acid contact.
5. Wash off any acid contact immediately and report it.
6. Connect and disconnect batteries only under supervision.
7. Do not smoke near batteries.
8. Report swelling, leaks or cracks in batteries.
9. Follow correct jump-start procedure only under supervision.
10. Keep battery area clean and dry.
Fuel and Fire Safety Rules
1. Do not smoke in the workshop.
2. Keep petrol and diesel away from flames.
3. Store fuel safely.
4. Clean fuel spills immediately.
5. Know where the fire extinguisher is kept.
6. Do not use open flames near vehicles.
7. Report fuel leaks immediately.
8. Do not pour fuel into open drains.
9. Dispose of oily rags safely.
10. Keep the workshop ventilated.
Vehicle Lifting Safety Rules
1. Do not lift a vehicle without supervision.
2. Use the correct jacking point.
3. Use axle stands before going under a vehicle.
4. Do not rely on a jack alone.
5. Use wheel chocks where needed.
6. Do not shake or climb a lifted vehicle.
7. Keep people away during lifting.
8. Lower vehicles slowly and carefully.
9. Report unsafe jacks or stands.
10. Do not work under a vehicle if unsure.
Road Testing Safety Rules
1. Trainees must not road test vehicles.
2. Only authorised persons may move or drive customer vehicles.
3. Do not sit in the driver’s seat without permission.
4. Do not start the engine without instruction.
5. Keep keys safe according to workshop rules.
6. Report any accidental movement or damage immediately.
Safety Scripture
Proverbs 22:3“A prudent man foreseeth the evil, and hideth himself: but the simple pass on, and are punished.”
Safety Character Lesson
Safety is wisdom. A disciplined mechanic trainee protects life, vehicles, tools, customers and the reputation of the Academy.

Motor Vehicle Mechanics is a practical and valuable field. Through good vehicle maintenance, families travel safely, businesses move goods, farmers reach markets, patients reach hospitals and communities remain connected.
