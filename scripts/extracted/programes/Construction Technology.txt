Dr Isa El-Buba Foundation (DISEF)
Departmental Training Module
Construction Technology

1. Department Overview
The Construction Technology Department trains young people to understand the basic principles, practices and discipline of construction work. It introduces trainees to building materials, site preparation, simple measurements, block work, concrete work, safety, teamwork, site organisation, basic drawings, finishing awareness and construction-related enterprise.
Construction is one of the most important fields for community development. Homes, schools, worship centres, hospitals, offices, roads, drainage systems and public buildings all require trained, disciplined and honest workers.
This department does not aim to turn trainees into master builders within seven months. Rather, it gives them a strong foundation so they can work safely under supervision, understand construction sites, assist skilled workers, continue learning and prepare for industrial attachment in building and construction-related environments.
The department also teaches trainees that construction work requires accuracy, patience, strength, teamwork, planning, discipline and integrity. A careless construction worker can waste materials, cause accidents and endanger lives. A faithful construction trainee must therefore learn to build with wisdom, safety and excellence.

2. Purpose of the Department
The purpose of the Construction Technology Department is to raise disciplined, skilled and God-fearing trainees who can participate responsibly in construction work, respect safety standards, support building projects and develop construction-related enterprise skills.
The department aims to:
1. Teach trainees the dignity and importance of construction work.
2. Introduce trainees to basic construction tools, materials and site activities.
3. Train trainees in simple measurement, levelling awareness, block work and concrete basics.
4. Teach safe handling of tools, materials and construction equipment.
5. Build discipline, accuracy, patience and teamwork.
6. Prepare trainees for industrial attachment in construction companies, building sites, workshops, project offices and related organisations.
7. Help trainees understand business opportunities in construction services.
8. Develop trainees who can serve communities through safe, honest and quality work.

8. Safety Rules
Construction work can be dangerous when tools, materials, heights, electricity, trenches, dust and heavy objects are handled carelessly.
General Site Safety Rules
1. Do not enter a construction area without permission.
2. Wear required protective equipment.
3. Do not run on site.
4. Do not throw tools or materials.
5. Do not stand under suspended loads.
6. Do not touch exposed wires.
7. Do not operate machines without training and permission.
8. Do not enter trenches without supervisor approval.
9. Do not climb unsafe structures.
10. Keep pathways clear.
11. Carry blocks and materials carefully.
12. Report injuries immediately.
13. Report unsafe tools.
14. Keep sharp objects away from walkways.
15. Store materials properly.
16. Wash hands after handling cement.
17. Avoid inhaling dust where possible.
18. Do not joke or fight during practical work.
19. Keep the work area clean.
20. Obey trainer and supervisor instructions at all times.
Cement and Concrete Safety
1. Avoid direct prolonged skin contact with cement.
2. Wear gloves where available.
3. Wash cement from skin quickly.
4. Do not allow cement dust into eyes.
5. Use masks where dust is heavy.
6. Keep cement dry.
7. Do not mix concrete without supervision.
8. Clean tools after use.
Tool Safety
1. Use the right tool for the right task.
2. Carry sharp tools safely.
3. Check tools before use.
4. Do not use broken tools.
5. Clean tools after work.
6. Return tools to storage.
7. Report lost or damaged tools.
Electrical Safety Awareness
1. Do not touch exposed wires.
2. Do not repair electrical faults unless trained and authorised.
3. Report electrical faults immediately.
4. Keep water away from electrical points.
5. Do not overload sockets or extension cables.
6. Treat all electrical hazards as dangerous.
Safety Scripture
Proverbs 22:3“A prudent man foreseeth the evil, and hideth himself: but the simple pass on, and are punished.”
Safety Character Lesson
Safety is wisdom. A disciplined trainee protects life, tools, materials and the reputation of the Academy.

Construction Technology is a powerful tool for character formation. It teaches planning, accuracy, patience, strength, teamwork and responsibility.

B. Planning

Planning prevents waste, confusion and abandoned projects.

Every strong building begins with a strong foundation.

Scripture
Ecclesiastes 4:9“Two are better than one; because they have a good reward for their labour.”
Character Lesson
Teamwork improves speed, safety and quality.

Wrong measurement can affect the whole building.
Scripture
Proverbs 11:1“A false balance is abomination to the LORD: but a just weight is his delight.”

Accuracy is part of honesty.

A responsible trainee protects materials and reduces waste.

Trainees should work as unto the Lord, not with eye-service.
