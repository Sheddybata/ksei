Departmental Training Module
Artificial Intelligence Awareness

1. Department Overview
The Artificial Intelligence Awareness Department trains young people to understand the basic meaning, uses, opportunities, dangers, ethics and practical applications of Artificial Intelligence in everyday life, work, ministry, education, business and community development.
Artificial Intelligence, often called AI, refers to computer systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as answering questions, generating text, recognising patterns, assisting with research, translating languages, analysing information, creating images, summarising documents and supporting decision-making.
AI is not God. AI is not the Holy Spirit. AI is not a replacement for wisdom, character, prayer, Scripture, human responsibility or professional judgement. AI is a tool. Like every tool, it can be used for good or misused for harm. The Academy must therefore train trainees to understand AI with wisdom, discipline, caution and Kingdom character.
This department does not aim to make trainees advanced AI engineers or data scientists within seven months. Rather, it gives them a strong foundation in AI awareness, responsible AI use, prompt writing, digital ethics, information checking, productivity, education support, business support, ministry support, workplace support, data privacy and AI-related enterprise opportunities.
AI awareness is important because the world of work is changing. Trainees who understand how to use AI responsibly can become more productive, more employable, more creative and better prepared for the future. However, trainees must also learn that AI can produce wrong information, biased information, false images, fake documents and misleading answers. Therefore, AI output must always be checked.
The Artificial Intelligence Awareness Department must train trainees to use AI for learning, service, creativity, problem-solving, productivity and community development, while avoiding deception, laziness, plagiarism, fraud, impersonation, misinformation and ungodly use.

2. Purpose of the Department
The purpose of the Artificial Intelligence Awareness Department is to raise disciplined, wise and God-fearing trainees who can understand and use AI tools responsibly for learning, work, enterprise, ministry and community impact.
The department aims to:
1. Teach trainees the basic meaning of Artificial Intelligence.
2. Help trainees understand the difference between human intelligence and machine assistance.
3. Train trainees in safe and responsible use of AI tools.
4. Teach trainees how to write clear prompts and ask good questions.
5. Build awareness of AI strengths, limitations and risks.
6. Train trainees to check AI-generated information before using it.
7. Teach AI ethics, data privacy, copyright, plagiarism and digital responsibility.
8. Show trainees how AI can support office work, education, agriculture, business, hospitality, finance, ministry and community development.
9. Prepare trainees for industrial attachment in digital, administrative, educational, media, business and technology-support environments.
10. Help trainees identify honest enterprise opportunities using AI-assisted services.
11. Develop trainees who can use AI with wisdom, humility, truth and Kingdom character.

8. Safety Rules
Artificial Intelligence Awareness includes ethical, digital, privacy, educational, spiritual and reputational risks. Trainees must use AI carefully and responsibly.
General AI Safety Rules
1. Do not share passwords with AI tools.
2. Do not enter private personal information without approval.
3. Do not enter confidential organisational records.
4. Do not enter financial records without supervision.
5. Do not enter medical information without proper authority.
6. Do not use AI to cheat in assignments or examinations.
7. Do not use AI to create fake documents.
8. Do not use AI to impersonate another person.
9. Do not use AI to spread false information.
10. Do not use AI for cybercrime.
11. Do not copy AI output blindly.
12. Do not treat AI answers as always correct.
13. Do not use AI to replace prayer, Scripture or spiritual counsel.
14. Do not use AI to replace qualified professional advice in serious matters.
15. Do not post AI-generated content without checking it.
16. Do not use AI to insult, manipulate or deceive people.
17. Do not create fake images or fake stories about people.
18. Do not share harmful or indecent content.
19. Report unsafe AI use to the trainer.
20. Obey trainer and supervisor instructions at all times.
Data Privacy Rules
1. Remove names when using examples.
2. Remove phone numbers, addresses and account details.
3. Do not upload confidential documents without approval.
4. Do not share student, client, patient, customer or ministry records.
5. Use fictional examples for practice.
6. Keep passwords private.
7. Log out after using shared devices.
8. Protect files from unauthorised access.
9. Ask permission before using another person’s content.
10. Report privacy mistakes immediately.
AI Output Checking Rules
1. Check dates.
2. Check names.
3. Check places.
4. Check figures.
5. Check Bible references.
6. Check legal or policy information with proper sources.
7. Check health information with qualified professionals.
8. Check financial calculations.
9. Check grammar and tone.
10. Check whether the output is suitable for the audience.
Academic and Workplace Integrity Rules
1. Use AI to learn, not to cheat.
2. Do not submit AI work as personal work without permission.
3. Give acknowledgement where required.
4. Rewrite in your own understanding.
5. Do not falsify reports.
6. Do not create fake certificates.
7. Do not create fake references.
8. Do not create fake receipts or invoices.
9. Do not mislead supervisors about AI use.
10. Maintain honesty in all digital work.
Spiritual and Character Safety Rules
1. Do not allow AI to replace God’s Word.
2. Do not allow AI to replace prayer.
3. Do not allow AI to replace obedience.
4. Do not use AI to promote pride.
5. Do not use AI to spread fear.
6. Do not use AI to attack people.
7. Do not use AI to manipulate emotions dishonestly.
8. Do not use AI to promote ungodly content.
9. Use AI to serve people.
10. Let wisdom and character guide every use of technology.
Safety Scripture
1 Thessalonians 5:21“Prove all things; hold fast that which is good.”
Safety Character Lesson
Responsible AI use requires testing, checking, wisdom and honesty. A disciplined trainee must not blindly trust or misuse AI.

17. Departmental Closing Charge
Artificial Intelligence Awareness is a timely and practical field. Through responsible AI use, trainees can learn faster, write better, organise information, support businesses, communicate clearly, serve ministries, solve problems and prepare for the future of work.
But AI must never be used without wisdom. It must not replace God, prayer, Scripture, character, human responsibility or professional judgement. It must not be used for fraud, laziness, deception, plagiarism, gossip, fake documents or harmful content.
The Academy is raising trainees who will use AI wisely, not foolishly. They must test information, protect privacy, honour truth, respect people, serve communities and remain faithful to God.
