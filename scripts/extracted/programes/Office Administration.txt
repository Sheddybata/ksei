Departmental Training Module
Office Administration
1. Department Overview
The Office Administration Department trains young people to understand the basic principles, skills, discipline, tools and character required to support the smooth running of offices, ministries, schools, businesses, organisations and public institutions.
Office administration involves organising information, handling documents, receiving visitors, managing communication, keeping records, arranging meetings, preparing reports, supporting supervisors, controlling files, managing schedules, protecting confidential information and ensuring that office work is done decently and in order.
Every organisation needs good administration. A business may have good ideas, but without administration, records become lost, meetings become confused, visitors are not attended to, documents are misplaced, instructions are forgotten and productivity is weakened. Good administration creates order, clarity, accountability and professional service.
This department does not aim to make trainees senior administrators within seven months. Rather, it gives trainees a strong foundation in office conduct, document preparation, filing, correspondence, reception, minute writing, scheduling, inventory awareness, digital office support, confidentiality, record keeping and workplace discipline.
An office administrator must be organised, respectful, accurate, confidential, punctual, neat, teachable and trustworthy. A careless office worker can expose private information, lose important files, embarrass an organisation, delay decisions or create confusion. Therefore, this department must train trainees to serve with excellence, humility and integrity.
2. Purpose of the Department
The purpose of the Office Administration Department is to raise disciplined, organised and God-fearing trainees who can support office operations with accuracy, confidentiality, order and excellent service.
The department aims to:
1. Teach trainees the meaning and importance of office administration.
2. Train trainees in professional office behaviour and workplace discipline.
3. Teach document preparation, filing, record keeping and correspondence.
4. Train trainees in reception duties, visitor handling and telephone etiquette.
5. Teach meeting preparation, agenda writing and minute taking.
6. Train trainees in scheduling, office inventory, simple reports and task follow-up.
7. Build confidentiality, accuracy, punctuality, respect and responsibility.
8. Prepare trainees for industrial attachment in offices, ministries, schools, businesses, NGOs, churches, hospitals, farms, hotels and public institutions.
9. Help trainees identify enterprise opportunities in administrative support services.
10. Develop trainees who can serve organisations with order, wisdom and Kingdom character.

8. Safety Rules
Office Administration may not involve heavy machines or farm animals, but it has serious ethical, information, document, electrical, posture and reputational risks. Trainees must handle records, visitors, devices and information carefully.
General Office Safety and Conduct Rules
1. Do not enter restricted offices without permission.
2. Do not open confidential files without authorisation.
3. Do not remove files without signing them out.
4. Do not disclose private information.
5. Do not gossip about office matters.
6. Do not falsify records.
7. Do not destroy documents without approval.
8. Do not sign documents you are not authorised to sign.
9. Do not misuse office equipment.
10. Do not use office time for laziness or idleness.
11. Do not eat or drink near official documents or computers.
12. Do not leave visitors unattended without guidance.
13. Do not argue with visitors.
