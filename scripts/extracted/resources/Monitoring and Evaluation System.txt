EBOMI KINGDOM LEADERSHIP ACADEMY
Monitoring and Evaluation System
For Spiritual Formation, Character, Skills, Employability, Enterprise and Community Impact
1. Introduction
Monitoring and Evaluation, often called M&E, is the system used to track progress, measure results, identify weaknesses, improve quality and confirm whether EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy is achieving its purpose.
The Academy is not only training students to attend classes. It is raising disciplined, spiritually grounded, skilled, employable, entrepreneurial, service-minded and nation-building young people. Therefore, the M&E system must measure both activities and transformation.
The system must measure:
Attendance.
Discipline.
Spiritual growth.
Technical competence.
Leadership ability.
Employability.
Entrepreneurial readiness.
Community service.
Industrial attachment performance.
The M&E system must answer these important questions:
Are trainees attending regularly?
Are trainees becoming more disciplined?
Are trainees growing spiritually?
Are trainees gaining practical skills?
Are trainees developing leadership ability?
Are trainees becoming employable?
Are trainees ready to start or support enterprises?
Are trainees serving the community?
Are trainees performing well during industrial attachment?
Is the Academy producing the kind of graduates it was established to raise?

3. M&E Philosophy
The M&E philosophy of EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy is based on the belief that what is not monitored may decline, and what is not evaluated may fail quietly.
The Academy shall therefore monitor progress continuously and evaluate results honestly.
Core M&E Principles
Wholistic Measurement: M&E must measure spiritual growth, character, academics, skills, leadership, work readiness and service.
Character Matters: Discipline, integrity and conduct must be tracked as seriously as technical skill.
Evidence-Based Reporting: Reports must be based on records, observation, practical evidence, supervisor feedback, logbooks and verified activities.
Continuous Improvement: M&E is not for blame alone. It is for correction, mentoring, improvement and better outcomes.
Practical Nigerian Context: The system must work even with limited resources, paper forms, simple spreadsheets and local supervision.
Shared Responsibility: Trainers, mentors, supervisors, management and trainees must all contribute to monitoring.
Transparency: Scores and reports must be honest, clear and traceable.
Accountability: Trainees, trainers and departments must be accountable for progress.
Transformation Focus: The Academy must not only count attendance. It must measure whether lives are changing.
Graduation Readiness: M&E must help management know who is truly ready for graduation, work, enterprise, ministry and community impact.

18. Final M&E Charge
Monitoring and Evaluation is not a mere administrative exercise. It is a stewardship responsibility. EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy must know whether trainees are attending, learning, changing, serving, growing, improving and becoming ready for life.
The Academy must not celebrate numbers only. It must celebrate transformation. It must not graduate trainees merely because time has passed. It must graduate trainees because evidence shows growth in character, competence, discipline, leadership, service and readiness.
