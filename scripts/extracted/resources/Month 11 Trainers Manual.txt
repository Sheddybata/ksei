EBOMI KINGDOM LEADERSHIP ACADEMY
365-Day Trainer’s Manual
Month 11
Monthly Theme: Leadership Practice, Employability and Enterprise Development
MONTH 11 OVERVIEW
Purpose of Month 11
Month 11 continues the industrial attachment phase with a stronger focus on leadership practice, employability and enterprise development. By this stage, trainees should have settled into their workplaces, improved their skills, accepted correction, taken responsibility and contributed to productivity.
Month 11 helps trainees move from being only learners to becoming responsible contributors who can lead by example, communicate well, work with others, solve problems, prepare for employment and begin thinking about enterprise opportunities.
This month does not mean trainees should become proud or take over their host organisations. It means they should practise leadership through humility, service, responsibility, excellence, communication and initiative.
Main Outcomes for Month 11
By the end of Month 11, trainees should be able to:
1. Practise leadership through service and example.
2. Demonstrate stronger employability behaviour.
3. Prepare for job search, interview and workplace transition.
4. Improve communication with supervisors, colleagues and customers.
5. Identify enterprise opportunities from attachment experience.
6. Understand how workplace learning can become business insight.
7. Prepare a simple career or enterprise development plan.
8. Demonstrate ethical leadership in real workplace situations.
9. Strengthen their attachment portfolio.
10. Receive supervisor feedback on leadership and employability.
11. Prepare for final attachment evaluation in Month 12.
12. Recommit to servant leadership, excellence and Kingdom integrity.
WEEK 41

DAY 281
Topic: Practising Leadership Without a Title
1. Learning Objectives
By the end of this lesson, trainees should be able to:
1. Understand that leadership does not always require a title.
2. Identify simple leadership opportunities during attachment.
3. Practise leadership through service.
4. Avoid pride and control.
5. Lead by example in daily workplace behaviour.
2. Scripture
Matthew 23:11
3. Memory Verse
1 Timothy 4:12
4. Teaching Notes
Leadership is not only a position. A person can lead through example, service, humility, responsibility and influence. During attachment, trainees may not have official titles, but they can still practise leadership.
A trainee practises leadership by:
1. Arriving early.
2. Completing tasks faithfully.
3. Helping others.
4. Speaking respectfully.
5. Keeping the workspace clean.
6. Reporting problems early.
7. Encouraging teamwork.
8. Accepting correction.
9. Doing small tasks well.
10. Protecting the name of the Academy.
11. Leadership without a title is powerful because it shows true character. If a trainee cannot serve without a title, he or she may misuse a title later.
5. Practical Activity
Each trainee should identify three ways to lead by example in the workplace without demanding any title.
6. Group Discussion
Discuss:
How can a trainee lead without being appointed as a leader?
7. Case Study
A trainee had no official position, but everyone respected him because he was punctual, humble, helpful and consistent. His example influenced other trainees positively.
8. Assessment
Assess through reflection, supervisor feedback and mentor observation.
9. Homework
Write one page on:
How I can practise leadership without a title during attachment.
10. Leadership Application
True leadership begins with example, not position.
11. Character Development Goal
Character Focus: Servant Leadership
Trainees should lead by serving faithfully and humbly.

Titus 2:7

Matthew 5:16
4. Teaching Notes
Leading by example means living in a way that others can safely follow. A trainee’s conduct should show discipline, respect, honesty and diligence.
A trainee can lead by example through:

4. Careful work.
5. Honest reporting.
6. Proper use of tools.

8. Good customer service.

Mark 10:45
3. Memory Verse
Philippians 2:3–4
4. Teaching Notes
Leadership must not become control. A trainee who is trusted with small responsibility should not become proud, harsh or bossy.

3. Serving before commanding.
4. Encouraging people.
5. Correcting respectfully.
6. Doing tasks with the team.
7. Protecting dignity.
8. Reporting truthfully.

10. Giving honour to others.

10. Leadership Application

Character Focus: Humility
Trainees should serve others instead of controlling them.
