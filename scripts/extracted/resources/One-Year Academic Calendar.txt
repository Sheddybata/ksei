EBOMI KINGDOM LEADERSHIP ACADEMY
One-Year Academic Calendar
Kingdom Leadership, Skills, Character and Nation-Building Programme
Nigeria

1. Introduction
The EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy one-year academic calendar is designed to guide the spiritual, academic, vocational, leadership and entrepreneurial development of every trainee.
The programme is divided into two major phases:
Phase One: Seven Months of Classroom Training with Daily Practicals
Phase Two: Five Months of Industrial Attachment
The first seven months will focus on classroom teaching, daily practical work, spiritual formation, character development, leadership training, vocational exposure and entrepreneurship preparation.
The final five months will focus on industrial attachment, workplace experience, practical competence, professional discipline, employability, business readiness and final evaluation.
The calendar is designed to ensure that every trainee grows in four major areas:
Kingdom Character
Professional Competence
Leadership Capacity
Entrepreneurial Ability
