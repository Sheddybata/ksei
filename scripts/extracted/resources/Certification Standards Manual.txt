TITLE PAGE
EBOMI KINGDOM LEADERSHIP ACADEMY
Certification Standards Manual
This manual defines the official standards for graduation, certification, grade classification, character clearance, practical competence, industrial attachment completion and alumni verification at EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy.
It is prepared for Academy management, trainers, mentors, departmental supervisors, industrial attachment supervisors, administrative officers, monitoring and evaluation officers, trainees, parents, guardians, partners and host organisations.
The certification system of the Academy is based on the full development of the trainee. No trainee shall be certified on academic performance alone. Character, discipline, integrity, attendance, punctuality, practical competence, teamwork, community service, portfolio evidence, logbook completion and industrial attachment performance shall count strongly.
A certificate from EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy must represent real training, real discipline, real character, real competence and real readiness for service.

1. INTRODUCTION
EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy exists to raise God-fearing, disciplined, skilled, responsible and service-minded young people who can serve God, build families, strengthen communities, create value and contribute to nation building.
Certification is therefore a serious matter. It is not a mere ceremony. It is not a reward for attending for one year only. It is an official confirmation that a trainee has met the Academy’s standards in character, competence, leadership, entrepreneurship, community service and workplace preparation.
The Academy recognises that a person may be intelligent but dishonest, talented but careless, skilful but rebellious, gifted but lazy or academically strong but morally weak. Such a person is not ready to represent the Academy well.
Therefore, the Academy’s certification system must protect the integrity of the programme.
2 Timothy 2:15“Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed.”
The Academy certifies trainees who have been approved through training, discipline, evidence and character.

2. PURPOSE OF CERTIFICATION
The purpose of certification is to confirm that a trainee has successfully completed the required training and has met the approved standards of the Academy.
Certification helps to:
1. Confirm that a trainee has completed the one-year programme.
2. Confirm that a trainee has met minimum attendance requirements.
3. Confirm that a trainee has participated in spiritual formation and character training.
4. Confirm that a trainee has demonstrated acceptable character and integrity.
5. Confirm that a trainee has completed classroom learning.
6. Confirm that a trainee has demonstrated practical competence.
7. Confirm that a trainee has participated in leadership and teamwork activities.
8. Confirm that a trainee has completed community service.
9. Confirm that a trainee has completed industrial attachment.
10. Confirm that a trainee has submitted portfolio and logbook evidence.
11. Confirm that a trainee is ready for employment, enterprise, apprenticeship, further training or community service.
12. Protect the reputation of the Academy.
13. Promote fairness, order and accountability.
3. MEANING OF EBOMI CERTIFICATION
EBOMI certification means that the Academy has reviewed the trainee’s records, conduct, attendance, performance, practical skills, community service, industrial attachment, portfolio, logbook and final assessment, and has found the trainee qualified according to the approved standard.
Certification means:
1. The trainee has passed through structured training.
2. The trainee has shown measurable growth.
3. The trainee has demonstrated practical learning.
4. The trainee has participated in community service.
5. The trainee has completed industrial attachment where required.
6. The trainee has received character clearance.
7. The trainee has submitted acceptable evidence of learning.
8. The trainee is considered fit to graduate according to Academy standards.
Certification does not mean that the trainee has reached perfection. It means the trainee has met the required standard and is ready for the next stage of life, work, enterprise, service or further learning.

4. CERTIFICATION PHILOSOPHY
The certification philosophy of EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy is based on four core outcomes.
4.1 Kingdom Character
The trainee must show evidence of honesty, discipline, humility, respect, purity, diligence, obedience, teachability, self-control, responsibility and fear of God.
4.2 Professional Competence
The trainee must demonstrate practical ability, workplace readiness, safety awareness, technical understanding, task completion, record keeping and quality of work.
4.3 Leadership Capacity
The trainee must show ability to serve, communicate, cooperate, solve problems, take responsibility, lead by example and work well with others.
4.4 Entrepreneurial Ability
The trainee must show ability to think productively, identify opportunities, plan small enterprise ideas, manage resources, keep simple records and practise financial integrity.
4.5 Certification Principle
A trainee must not be certified by academic marks alone. Certification must consider the whole person.
The Academy must ask:
1. Is this trainee faithful?
2. Is this trainee teachable?
3. Is this trainee honest?
4. Is this trainee disciplined?
5. Is this trainee practically competent?
6. Is this trainee safe to recommend?
7. Is this trainee responsible in community service?
8. Is this trainee ready for industrial or workplace responsibility?
9. Is this trainee a good ambassador of the Academy?
10. Is this trainee fit to receive certification?

6. CERTIFICATE CATEGORIES
The Academy may issue different certificate categories according to the trainee’s performance, conduct and completion status.
The certificate category must be based on evidence and approved by the Graduation Clearance Committee.

7. CERTIFICATE OF COMPLETION
A Certificate of Completion may be issued to a trainee who has completed the required duration of the programme and met the basic graduation requirements.
7.1 Requirements
The trainee must have:
1. Completed Phase One classroom and practical training.
2. Completed Phase Two industrial attachment or approved equivalent.
3. Met minimum attendance requirement.
4. Submitted acceptable portfolio and logbook.
5. Completed community service requirement.
6. Participated in final interview or presentation.
7. Received character clearance.
8. Cleared all major Academy obligations.
9. Had no unresolved serious misconduct.
7.2 Meaning
This certificate confirms that the trainee completed the full programme and met the Academy’s minimum standards for graduation.

8. CERTIFICATE OF COMPETENCE
A Certificate of Competence may be issued to a trainee who has completed the programme and demonstrated strong practical competence in a specific department or skill area.
8.1 Requirements
The trainee must have:
1. Met all requirements for Certificate of Completion.
2. Achieved the required practical skills standard.
3. Completed departmental practical assessments.
4. Submitted practical evidence in the portfolio.
5. Completed industrial attachment satisfactorily.
6. Received recommendation from the departmental supervisor.
7. Demonstrated safety awareness.
8. Demonstrated ability to perform selected tasks under reduced supervision.
9. Received character clearance.
8.2 Meaning
This certificate confirms that the trainee has not only completed the programme, but has also demonstrated practical competence in the approved department or skill area.
8.3 Important Note
A trainee cannot receive a Certificate of Competence if practical competence is weak, unsafe or not properly evidenced, even if academic scores are high.

9. CERTIFICATE OF PARTICIPATION
A Certificate of Participation may be issued to a trainee who participated in part of the programme but did not meet the full requirements for graduation or competence certification.
9.1 When It May Be Issued
It may be issued when a trainee:
1. Attended a recognised portion of the programme.
2. Participated in selected training activities.
3. Did not complete all requirements for graduation.
4. Did not complete industrial attachment.
5. Did not submit full portfolio or logbook.
6. Was officially withdrawn before completion.
7. Was part of a special short-term training approved by management.
9.2 Limitation
A Certificate of Participation must not be presented as proof of graduation or competence.

10. DEFERRED CERTIFICATION
Deferred Certification means the trainee is not certified immediately, but may be certified later after completing required corrections, evidence, remediation or clearance.
10.1 Reasons for Deferred Certification
Certification may be deferred if:
1. Portfolio is incomplete.
2. Logbook is incomplete.
3. Industrial attachment report is missing.
4. Community service requirement is incomplete.
5. Attendance record needs management review.
6. Practical skills require reassessment.
7. Character concern requires mentoring or restoration.
8. Final report is not submitted.
9. Final interview or presentation is not completed.
10. Academy property, tools or documents are not cleared.
11. Fees, materials or approved obligations remain unresolved, where applicable.
10.2 Deferred Certification Rule
Deferred Certification should state clearly what the trainee must correct and the deadline for correction.

11. NON-CERTIFICATION
Non-Certification means the Academy cannot certify the trainee.
11.1 Reasons for Non-Certification
A trainee may not be certified if:
1. The trainee did not complete the programme.
2. Attendance was far below the minimum standard.
3. Character clearance was denied.
4. Serious misconduct was unresolved.
5. Practical competence was unsafe or unacceptable.
6. Industrial attachment was not completed without approval.
7. Portfolio and logbook were falsified.
8. Final assessment was failed without remediation.
9. The trainee abandoned the programme.
10. The trainee brought serious disrepute to the Academy.
11.2 Non-Certification Rule
Non-Certification must be based on records, evidence, fair review and management approval.

13. ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENT
Attendance is a major part of certification.
13.1 Attendance Standard
A trainee must have at least 85% overall attendance to be considered for graduation, unless Academy management approves an exception due to serious medical, family or official reasons.
13.2 Attendance Areas
Attendance shall include:
1. Morning devotion.
2. Bible study.
3. Classroom lectures.
4. Practical training.
5. Departmental work.
6. Mentoring sessions.
7. Community service.
8. Evening reflection where applicable.
9. Industrial attachment.
13.3 Attendance Warning
Repeated absence without approval may block certification.
13.4 Punctuality
Repeated late coming shows indiscipline and may affect character clearance, even where total attendance is acceptable.
Proverbs 6:6“Go to the ant, thou sluggard; consider her ways, and be wise.”

14. ACADEMIC REQUIREMENT
Academic learning is important, but it is not the only basis for certification.
14.1 Academic Evidence
Academic assessment may include:
1. Class assignments.
2. Tests.
3. Quizzes.
4. Group work.
5. Case study responses.
6. Written reflections.
7. Presentations.
8. Final written assessment where applicable.
14.2 Minimum Standard
A trainee should score at least 50% average in academic learning to be considered for graduation.
14.3 Academic Warning
A trainee with strong character and practical skills but weak academics may be given remediation. A trainee with strong academics but poor character must not be certified until character clearance is resolved.

15. PRACTICAL SKILLS REQUIREMENT
Practical competence is central to the Academy.
15.1 Practical Assessment Areas
Trainees shall be assessed in:
1. Understanding instructions.
2. Tool and material handling.
3. Safety compliance.
4. Step-by-step procedure.
5. Task completion.
6. Quality of work.
7. Ability to correct mistakes.
8. Cleanliness and organisation.
9. Teamwork during practical work.
10. Ability to explain the task.
15.2 Minimum Standard
A trainee must achieve at least a satisfactory practical rating to graduate.
For a Certificate of Competence, the trainee must demonstrate good or excellent practical ability in the department or approved skill area.
15.3 Unsafe Performance
A trainee who performs practical tasks unsafely must not receive competence certification until retraining and reassessment are completed.
Exodus 35:35“Them hath he filled with wisdom of heart, to work all manner of work.”
Skill must be joined with wisdom.

28. CERTIFICATE APPROVAL PROCEDURE
Certification must follow an orderly approval process.
28.1 Steps
1. Department submits final performance records.
2. Trainer submits academic and practical scores.
3. Mentor submits character and growth report.
4. Community service officer submits service clearance.
5. Industrial attachment officer submits attachment clearance.
6. Admin officer submits attendance and punctuality records.
7. M&E officer reviews portfolio, logbook and evidence.
8. Graduation Clearance Committee reviews the trainee.
9. Character Gate decision is made.
10. Final score is confirmed.
11. Certificate category is recommended.
12. Management approves or rejects recommendation.
13. Certificate register is updated.
14. Certificate is prepared.
15. Certificate is signed by authorised officers.
16. Certificate is issued at graduation or approved collection date.
28.2 Approval Rule
No certificate should be printed or issued before final approval is recorded.

34. ALUMNI VERIFICATION PROCEDURE
Alumni verification is the process of confirming that a person truly graduated from the Academy or received a recognised certificate.
34.1 Verification May Be Requested By
1. Graduate.
2. Employer.
3. Training institution.
4. Host organisation.
5. Partner organisation.
6. Government or community body.
7. Scholarship or sponsorship body.
8. Academy management.
34.2 Information Needed for Verification
The requester should provide:
1. Full name of graduate.
2. Student ID where available.
3. Certificate number.
4. Department.
5. Year of graduation.
6. Copy or picture of certificate where available.
7. Purpose of verification.
8. Contact details of requester.
9. 3 Verification Steps
10. Receive verification request.
11. Confirm identity of requester where necessary.
12. Check certificate register.
13. Confirm certificate number.
14. Confirm student name and department.
15. Confirm year of graduation.
16. Confirm certificate category.
17. Confirm grade where authorised.
18. Prepare official verification response.
19. Record verification request.
34.4 Verification Response
The Academy may confirm:
1. Whether the certificate is valid.
2. Certificate category.
3. Department.
4. Year of graduation.
5. Grade classification where approved.
6. Any limitation if certificate was participation only.
34.5 Confidentiality Rule
The Academy must not release private disciplinary, welfare, medical or personal records without proper authority.

35. REPLACEMENT OF CERTIFICATE POLICY
A replacement certificate may be issued only under approved conditions.
35.1 Reasons for Replacement
A replacement may be considered if:
1. Original certificate is lost.
2. Original certificate is damaged.
3. Name correction is approved.
4. Printing error is confirmed by the Academy.
5. Certificate was stolen and evidence is provided.
35.2 Requirements for Replacement
The applicant may be required to provide:
1. Written application.
2. Full name.
3. Student ID.
4. Certificate number where known.
5. Year of graduation.
6. Department.
7. Evidence of loss or damage.
8. Identification.
9. Police report where required by management.
10. Replacement fee where approved.
35.3 Replacement Certificate Marking
Replacement certificates should be marked:
Replacement Copy
35.4 Replacement Register
All replacement certificates must be entered in the certificate register with date and reason for replacement.

37. FINAL CHARGE TO GRADUATES
Dear graduate,
A certificate is not the end of training. It is a call to responsibility.
Do not carry the certificate without carrying the character.Do not celebrate the certificate and forget the discipline.Do not claim competence without continuing to learn.Do not use your skill to deceive people.Do not use your training to oppress others.Do not forget the God who helped you.Do not forget the community you are called to serve.
Your certificate must speak of truth.Your life must speak of integrity.Your hands must speak of skill.Your speech must speak of wisdom.Your service must speak of humility.Your leadership must speak of responsibility.
Proverbs 22:1“A good name is rather to be chosen than great riches, and loving favour rather than silver and gold.”
Protect your good name. Protect the name of your family. Protect the name of EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy. Above all, honour the name of the Lord.
Matthew 5:16“Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works.”
You have been raised to serve.You have been trained to build.You are being sent to lead.
Serve God.Serve people.Build your community.Reject corruption.Walk in integrity.Keep learning.Start small where necessary.Work diligently.Lead humbly.Use your certificate as a door to service, not as a crown of pride.
Final Scripture
Psalm 90:17“And let the beauty of the LORD our God be upon us: and establish thou the work of our hands upon us; yea, the work of our hands establish thou it.”
May the Lord establish the work of your hands and make your life a true certificate of Kingdom character, professional competence, leadership capacity and entrepreneurial ability.
