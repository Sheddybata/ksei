EBOMI KINGDOM LEADERSHIP ACADEMY
Master Curriculum Framework
One-Year Kingdom Leadership, Skills, Character and Nation-Building Programme
Nigeria

1. Introduction
EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy is a one-year transformational training programme designed to raise disciplined, skilled, productive, God-fearing and responsible young men and women who can contribute meaningfully to society.
The Academy exists to address more than unemployment. Its deeper purpose is to build people. Many young people have certificates but lack direction, discipline, practical skills, godly values, workplace readiness and leadership capacity. EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy is designed to close that gap.
The programme combines spiritual formation, leadership development, vocational training, entrepreneurship, industrial attachment, community service and character development. It is not only an academic programme; it is a life-shaping academy.
At the end of the programme, every trainee should be able to demonstrate godly character, practical competence, leadership capacity and entrepreneurial ability.

2. Vision
To raise a generation of dependable, disciplined, skilled, productive and God-fearing leaders who will transform communities, institutions, industries and nations through Kingdom character, professional excellence and servant leadership.

3. Mission
The mission of EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy is to train unemployed graduates, secondary school leavers, orphans, vulnerable young people and other promising individuals to become spiritually mature, morally upright, professionally competent, entrepreneurial and ready to serve society with excellence.
The Academy seeks to prepare trainees to become:
Faithful servants of God.
Responsible citizens.
Skilled workers.
Job creators.
Ethical public servants.
Excellent professionals.
Community builders.
Ambassadors of Christ in society.

4. Programme Philosophy
The philosophy of EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy is built on the belief that true education must transform the whole person.
The Academy does not exist only to transfer knowledge. It exists to shape character, build competence, develop leadership and prepare trainees for productive service.
Every aspect of the programme must produce four outcomes:
Kingdom Character
Professional Competence
Leadership Capacity
Entrepreneurial Ability
The programme believes that skill without character is dangerous, leadership without integrity is destructive, and knowledge without responsibility is incomplete.
Therefore, no trainee should graduate only because he or she passed academic tests. Trainees must also demonstrate discipline, punctuality, honesty, respect, accountability, teamwork, diligence, humility and service.
The Academy will emphasise:
Practical learning over excessive theory.
Demonstration over mere explanation.
Mentorship over distant instruction.
Discipline over casual participation.
Service before leadership.
Character before certificate.
Excellence in every assignment.
Faithfulness in little things.

5. Target Participants
The programme is designed for young men and women who require direction, discipline, skills and leadership formation.
Target participants include:
Unemployed graduates.
Secondary school leavers.
Orphans and vulnerable young people.
Youths from low-income families.
Young people with leadership potential but limited opportunities.
Young people interested in vocational and technical skills.
Young people preparing for employment, entrepreneurship, ministry or public service.
Young people who need character formation, mentoring and life direction.
Participants must be willing to learn, submit to discipline, participate in practical work, respect authority, attend devotions and complete all academic, spiritual, practical and community service requirements.

6. Programme Duration
The programme shall run for one full year, divided into two major phases:
Phase One: Seven Months of Classroom Training with Daily Practicals
This phase will focus on spiritual formation, leadership development, classroom teaching, technical exposure, daily practical assignments, mentoring and character development.
Phase Two: Five Months of Industrial Attachment
This phase will focus on workplace experience, field deployment, supervised practice, professional discipline, employability, entrepreneurship and real-life application.

7. Phase One: Seven Months of Classroom Training with Daily Practicals
The first seven months shall provide intensive classroom instruction combined with daily practical work.
The aim of this phase is to build a strong foundation before trainees are deployed into industries, organisations, ministries, farms, workshops, offices and other professional environments.
7.1 Objectives of Phase One
By the end of the seven-month classroom and practical phase, trainees should be able to:
Understand the vision and values of the Academy.
Demonstrate basic spiritual discipline.
Practise punctuality, obedience, diligence and accountability.
Understand biblical leadership and servant leadership.
Develop foundational professional skills.
Participate actively in departmental practical work.
Understand basic entrepreneurship and financial discipline.
Work effectively in teams.
Communicate clearly and respectfully.
Prepare for industrial attachment.
7.2 Daily Training Structure
The daily programme shall include:
Personal preparation.
Morning devotion, worship, prayer, Bible study and character charge.
Physical exercise and environmental sanitation.
Classroom lectures.
Technical and professional training.
Departmental work and practical assignments.
Mentoring and leadership discussion.
Assignments, reading and group work.
Evening prayer, reflection and accountability report.
7.3 Main Learning Areas in Phase One
The seven-month classroom phase shall cover:
Kingdom character and integrity.
Biblical leadership.
Spiritual discipline.
Communication skills.
Personal financial management.
Entrepreneurship.
ICT and digital skills.
Artificial intelligence awareness.
Technical and vocational training.
Community development.
Nation building.
Public service ethics.
Practical departmental work.
Teamwork and leadership.
Preparation for industrial attachment.
7.4 Daily Practicals
Every trainee must participate in supervised practical work every day. These practicals may take place in:
Farms.
Workshops.
Construction sites.
Offices.
ICT laboratories.
Hospitality units.
Finance departments.
Mechanical workshops.
Welding and fabrication areas.
Administrative departments.
Community development sites.
The practical session must not be treated as free labour. It must be structured as supervised training. Every practical session should have a learning objective, assigned task, supervisor, safety instruction and evaluation.

8. Phase Two: Five Months of Industrial Attachment
The second phase of the programme shall focus on industrial attachment and field deployment.
Each trainee shall be placed in a reputable organisation, company, ministry, business, farm, workshop or institution according to his or her area of training and specialisation.
8.1 Objectives of Phase Two
By the end of the five-month industrial attachment, trainees should be able to:
Apply classroom knowledge in real workplace settings.
Demonstrate punctuality and professional discipline.
Work under supervision.
Follow workplace rules and safety standards.
Relate respectfully with employers, supervisors, colleagues and customers.
Solve practical problems.
Write weekly reports.
Receive and apply feedback.
Demonstrate employability.
Identify business and career opportunities.
8.2 Possible Placement Areas
Trainees may be placed in:
Construction companies.
Engineering firms.
Commercial farms.
Hotels.
Restaurants.
Financial institutions.
ICT companies.
Mechanical workshops.
Government ministries.
Manufacturing industries.
Transport companies.
Media organisations.
Educational institutions.
Civil society organisations.
Community development projects.
8.3 Industrial Attachment Requirements
During industrial attachment:
Every trainee must report to the assigned workplace as required.
Every trainee must maintain punctuality and discipline.
Every trainee must submit a weekly report.
Workplace supervisors must complete monthly evaluation forms.
EBOMI mentors must visit trainees regularly.
Trainees must complete a final industrial attachment report.
Trainees must demonstrate good character throughout the placement.
Any serious misconduct may affect graduation eligibility.

12. Graduation Expectations
A trainee shall be considered ready for graduation when he or she has demonstrated growth in character, competence, leadership and entrepreneurship.
12.1 Minimum Graduation Requirements
To graduate, every trainee must:
Complete the required duration of the programme.
Meet the minimum attendance requirement.
Complete the seven-month classroom and practical training phase.
Complete the five-month industrial attachment phase.
Submit all required assignments and reports.
Participate in community service.
Pass practical skills assessments.
Demonstrate acceptable Kingdom character.
Complete the entrepreneurship project or business plan.
Receive satisfactory industrial attachment evaluation.
Show evidence of discipline, respect and responsibility.
Meet the Academy’s certification standards.
12.2 Trainees Who Should Not Graduate Automatically
A trainee should not graduate automatically if he or she:
Shows repeated dishonesty.
Refuses correction.
Has poor attendance.
Is repeatedly late.
Demonstrates indiscipline.
Fails practical assessments.
Shows disrespect to trainers or supervisors.
Refuses community service.
Fails to complete industrial attachment.
Damages the name or values of the Academy.
Such trainees may be required to repeat parts of the programme, undergo counselling, complete additional service or receive delayed certification.

13. Certification Framework
Certification shall reflect both competence and character.
Recommended certification categories:
Certificate of CompletionFor trainees who complete the programme and meet minimum requirements.
Certificate of MeritFor trainees who perform above average in academics, practical work and character.
Certificate of DistinctionFor trainees who demonstrate excellent performance across all major areas.
Character Excellence AwardFor trainees who demonstrate exceptional integrity, discipline, humility, service and consistency.
Leadership Excellence AwardFor trainees who demonstrate outstanding leadership, teamwork and responsibility.
Entrepreneurship Excellence AwardFor trainees who present strong, practical and realistic business or enterprise plans.
Community Service AwardFor trainees who show exceptional commitment to community transformation.

18. Conclusion
EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy is designed to become a model of Kingdom-based leadership, skills and character development in Nigeria and beyond.
The Academy is not merely producing workers. It is raising builders of society. It is not merely offering training. It is shaping lives. It is not merely giving certificates. It is preparing men and women who can be trusted with responsibility.
Through this one-year programme, trainees shall be formed spiritually, trained professionally, developed as leaders and prepared for entrepreneurship. The final goal is transformation: transformed lives, transformed communities, transformed institutions and a transformed nation.
The standard of the Academy shall be excellence, integrity, discipline, service and Kingdom impact.
