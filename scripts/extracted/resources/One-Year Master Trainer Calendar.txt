How to Use This One-Year Master Trainer Calendar
This document converts the EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy programme into a complete one-year lesson map. It does not attempt to write a long expanded lesson for every single day. Instead, it gives every trainer the exact day, date, week, programme month, monthly theme, weekly focus, daily topic and main activity for the whole year.
The standard daily lesson plan template should be used whenever a trainer needs to expand any daily topic into a full lesson. This approach is practical, faster, easier to manage and more suitable for a full-year academy.
Phase One covers seven months of classroom training with daily practicals. Phase Two covers five months of industrial attachment. Character, competence, leadership and entrepreneurship are assessed throughout the year.
