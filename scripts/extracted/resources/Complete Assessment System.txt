EBOMI KINGDOM LEADERSHIP ACADEMY
Complete Assessment System
1. Assessment Philosophy
The assessment system of EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy is designed to measure the whole person, not only academic knowledge or technical skill. The Academy exists to raise trainees who are spiritually grounded, morally sound, disciplined, skilled, productive, employable, service-minded and ready to contribute to society.
Therefore, assessment must cover the following areas:
Academic performance.
Practical skills.
Character.
Integrity.
Attendance.
Punctuality.
Leadership.
Teamwork.
Communication.
Problem solving.
Creativity.
Community service.
Industrial attachment performance.
The Academy believes that a skilful person without character is dangerous, and a person of character without competence may be limited in productivity. Therefore, the assessment system must give equal seriousness to Character and Integrity and Practical/Technical Competence.
Foundational Scripture
Luke 16:10
Quoted Scripture:“He that is faithful in that which is least is faithful also in much.”
Assessment Lesson:Faithfulness in small duties, daily conduct, punctuality, honesty and obedience shows whether a trainee can be trusted with greater responsibility.
Proverbs 22:29
Quoted Scripture:“Seest thou a man diligent in his business? he shall stand before kings; he shall not stand before mean men.”
Assessment Lesson:Diligence in training prepares trainees for honourable service and future opportunities.
1 Corinthians 4:2
Quoted Scripture:“Moreover it is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful.”
Assessment Lesson:Every trainee is a steward of time, opportunity, gifts, tools, relationships and training resources.
2 Timothy 2:15
Quoted Scripture:“Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed.”
Assessment Lesson:The Academy must produce workers who are approved in knowledge, character, skill and service.
2. Assessment Principles
The assessment system shall be guided by the following principles:
Wholistic Assessment: Trainees shall be assessed in knowledge, skill, character, service and workplace readiness.
Character Equality: Character and integrity shall carry the same weight as practical and technical competence.
Continuous Assessment: Assessment shall be weekly, monthly, termly, during industrial attachment and at graduation.
Evidence-Based Assessment: Scores must be based on observable conduct, submitted work, practical demonstrations, attendance records, supervisor reports and verified evidence.
Fairness: All trainees shall be assessed by clear standards.
Correction and Growth: Assessment is not only for punishment but for growth, correction, mentoring and transformation.
Integrity Protection: Cheating, stealing, violence, sexual misconduct, falsification of records, bribery, serious dishonesty or repeated rebellion may affect graduation.
Practical Readiness: No trainee should graduate without demonstrating practical competence.
Service Orientation: Community service shall be part of assessment because trainees are being raised to serve society.
Attachment Accountability: Industrial attachment shall be assessed by both the host supervisor and the Academy.

4. Minimum Graduation Requirements
To graduate from EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy, a trainee must:
Score at least 50% overall.
Score at least 50% in Character and Integrity.
Score at least 50% in Practical and Technical Skills.
Complete required community service.
Complete industrial attachment.
Submit required portfolio, logbook and reports.
Have no unresolved serious disciplinary or integrity case.
Demonstrate readiness for work, enterprise, ministry or further training.
Character Gate Rule
A trainee may pass academic and practical assessment but still fail graduation readiness if there is serious unresolved evidence of:
Stealing.
Violence.
Sexual misconduct.
Examination malpractice.
Falsification of records.
Repeated lying.
Repeated rebellion against authority.
Bullying or harassment.
Destruction of Academy property.
Serious misconduct during industrial attachment.

14. Academy Assessment Committee
The Academy should establish an Assessment Committee to review final scores and graduation readiness.
Suggested Members
Academy Director.
Academic Coordinator.
Spiritual/Character Formation Coordinator.
Departmental Trainer.
Industrial Attachment Coordinator.
Community Service Coordinator.
Administrative Officer.
Mentor or House Parent where applicable.
Responsibilities
The Committee shall:
Review all assessment records.
Confirm score accuracy.
Review character and integrity reports.
Review industrial attachment reports.
Review community service records.
Confirm completion of logbooks and portfolios.
Decide remediation where needed.
Approve graduation list.
Recommend special awards.
Protect fairness and integrity in the assessment process.

18. Final Charge on Assessment
Assessment at EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy must never be reduced to marks alone. Marks are important, but transformation is greater. The Academy must assess what a trainee knows, what a trainee can do, how a trainee behaves, how a trainee serves, how a trainee handles responsibility and how a trainee represents Christ in the workplace and community.
A trainee who graduates from the Academy should be:
Skilled in practical work.
Sound in basic knowledge.
Strong in character.
Honest in conduct.
Faithful in attendance.
Punctual in duty.
Respectful in communication.
Responsible in leadership.
Helpful in teamwork.
Creative in problem solving.
Humble in service.
Ready for work, enterprise, ministry and nation building.
