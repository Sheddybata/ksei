1. INTRODUCTION TO THE WORKBOOK
This workbook has been prepared to help every trainee of EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy grow spiritually, mentally, morally, practically and professionally.
You are not in the Academy only to listen to lessons. You are here to think, reflect, practise, write, improve, serve and prepare for life.
This workbook will help you to:
1. Reflect on your spiritual growth.
2. Record your daily devotion lessons.
3. Answer Bible study questions.
4. Track Scripture memorisation.
5. Reflect on character development.
6. Complete academic exercises.
7. Record practical skills.
8. Reflect on demonstrations.
9. Prepare for group discussions.
10. Respond to case studies.
11. Develop leadership and teamwork.
12. Practise communication.
13. Solve problems.
14. Develop business ideas.
15. Prepare for employment.
16. Prepare for industrial attachment.
17. Reflect on your final transformation.
18. Prepare for graduation.
James 1:22“But be ye doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving your own selves.”
This workbook will help you become a doer, not a hearer only.

2. HOW STUDENTS SHOULD USE THE WORKBOOK
Use this workbook daily and honestly.
Instructions
1. Bring this workbook to approved classes, mentoring sessions and practical reviews.
2. Write clearly.
3. Write the truth.
4. Do not copy another student’s answers.
5. Complete reflections regularly.
6. Submit pages for trainer or mentor review when required.
7. Use the workbook to track your growth.
8. Keep the workbook neat and safe.
9. Add corrections when your trainer or mentor gives feedback.
10. Use your answers to build your final portfolio.
Luke 16:10“He that is faithful in that which is least is faithful also in much.”
Faithfulness in small daily reflections prepares you for greater responsibility.

34. FINAL CHARGE
Dear trainee,
This workbook is not only a book of questions. It is a mirror of your journey.
It shows what you are learning.It shows how you are thinking.It shows how you are growing.It shows how you are responding to correction.It shows how you are preparing for responsibility.
Do not only fill pages. Become the person God wants you to become.
Let your answers be honest.Let your reflections be deep.Let your practical work be diligent.Let your character become stronger.Let your service become humble.Let your leadership become responsible.Let your skill become useful.Let your life bring glory to God.
Psalm 90:17“And let the beauty of the LORD our God be upon us: and establish thou the work of our hands upon us; yea, the work of our hands establish thou it.”
May the Lord establish the work of your hands and make you a faithful servant, skilled worker, humble leader and nation builder.
