TITLE PAGE
EBOMI KINGDOM LEADERSHIP ACADEMY
Student Handbook
This handbook is prepared for all trainees of EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy.
It explains the vision, mission, rules, expectations, spiritual formation, daily discipline, academic structure, practical training requirements, character standards, industrial attachment expectations, graduation requirements and student commitment of the Academy.
Every trainee must read this handbook carefully, understand it, obey it and sign the Student Commitment Pledge at the end.
EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy is not only a place of learning. It is a place of transformation. It is a place where young people are trained to love God, fear God, serve people, develop useful skills, lead with integrity, work with diligence and contribute to the rebuilding of communities and the nation.

1. WELCOME MESSAGE
Dear trainee,
You are welcome to EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy.
Your admission into this Academy is a great opportunity. It is not just an opportunity to learn a skill. It is an opportunity to be shaped, disciplined, corrected, trained, mentored and prepared for a life of usefulness.
Many young people desire a better future, but not everyone is willing to submit to the process that produces that future. At EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy, we believe that God can raise ordinary young people and use them for extraordinary service when they are teachable, disciplined, obedient and diligent.
This Academy exists to train unemployed graduates, secondary school leavers, orphans, vulnerable young people and others who need spiritual formation, vocational competence, leadership development, entrepreneurship, discipline, community service and practical work experience.
You are not here by accident. You are here to be trained. You are here to be corrected. You are here to be prepared. You are here to discover purpose, build character and become useful to God, your family, your community and your nation.
The Academy will be firm, but fatherly. We will teach you, but we will also discipline you. We will encourage you, but we will also correct you. We will pray with you, but we will also expect you to work hard. We will care for you, but we will also expect you to take responsibility.
The Scripture says:
Proverbs 22:29“Seest thou a man diligent in his business? he shall stand before kings; he shall not stand before mean men.”
Diligence will open doors for you. Discipline will preserve you. Character will sustain you. Skill will make you useful. The fear of God will keep you from destruction.
You are welcome to a season of training, transformation and preparation.

2. VISION OF THE ACADEMY
The vision of EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy is to raise a generation of God-fearing, disciplined, skilled, responsible and service-minded young leaders who will become agents of transformation in their families, communities, workplaces, ministries and nations.
The Academy exists to raise young people who will not only seek personal success, but will also carry a burden for community development, national transformation and Kingdom service.
Matthew 5:14“Ye are the light of the world. A city that is set on an hill cannot be hid.”
The Academy believes that every trainee must become light, not darkness. Every trainee must become a solution, not a problem. Every trainee must become a builder, not a destroyer.

3. MISSION OF THE ACADEMY
The mission of EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy is to provide spiritual formation, character training, leadership development, vocational skills, entrepreneurship education, community service experience and industrial exposure for young people, especially those who need direction, discipline, empowerment and practical preparation for life.
The Academy will fulfil this mission through:
1. Daily spiritual formation.
2. Classroom teaching.
3. Practical vocational training.
4. Character development.
5. Leadership training.
6. Entrepreneurship education.
7. Community service.
8. Mentorship and counselling.
9. Industrial attachment.
10. Continuous assessment and accountability.
Colossians 3:23“And whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord, and not unto men.”
Every trainee must learn to work heartily, faithfully and excellently.

6. CORE TRAINING OUTCOMES
The Academy expects every trainee to grow in five major outcomes.

6.1 Kingdom Character
Kingdom character means living under the fear of God, obeying His Word and reflecting Christ in daily behaviour.
A trainee with Kingdom character must show:
1. Honesty.
2. Purity.
3. Humility.
4. Obedience.
5. Respect.
6. Forgiveness.
7. Self-control.
8. Faithfulness.
9. Truthfulness.
10. Compassion.
Galatians 5:22-23“But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith,Meekness, temperance: against such there is no law.”
Character is more important than talent. A skilled person without character can become dangerous. The Academy will therefore take character training very seriously.

6.2 Professional Competence
Professional competence means the ability to perform assigned duties with knowledge, skill, accuracy, discipline and excellence.
Every trainee must learn to:
1. Understand instructions.
2. Complete tasks properly.
3. Work safely.
4. Keep records.
5. Use tools correctly.
6. Respect supervisors.
7. Improve through practice.
8. Avoid careless work.
9. Ask questions when unsure.
10. Deliver work that can be trusted.
Ecclesiastes 9:10“Whatsoever thy hand findeth to do, do it with thy might.”
The Academy expects trainees to work with strength, attention and excellence.

6.3 Leadership Capacity
Leadership capacity means the ability to influence, organise, serve, guide and take responsibility.
A good leader must first learn to serve. Leadership at the Academy is not pride, control or popularity. It is responsibility.
Every trainee must learn to:
1. Serve others.
2. Follow before leading.
3. Communicate clearly.
4. Take responsibility.
5. Solve problems.
6. Work with teams.
7. Respect authority.
8. Make wise decisions.
9. Handle conflict peacefully.
10. Lead by example.
Mark 10:45“For even the Son of man came not to be ministered unto, but to minister.”
The leadership model of the Academy is servant leadership.

6.4 Entrepreneurial Ability
Entrepreneurial ability means the capacity to identify opportunities, solve problems, create value, manage resources and build honest income-generating activities.
Every trainee must learn:
1. Business thinking.
2. Customer service.
3. Basic financial discipline.
4. Saving culture.
5. Costing and pricing.
6. Record keeping.
7. Marketing.
8. Problem-solving.
9. Starting small.
10. Growing with integrity.
Deuteronomy 8:18“But thou shalt remember the LORD thy God: for it is he that giveth thee power to get wealth.”
The Academy teaches that wealth must be created with honesty, diligence and the fear of God.

6.5 Nation-Building Mindset
A nation-building mindset means thinking beyond personal comfort. It means understanding that every trainee has a role in rebuilding families, communities and the nation.
Trainees must learn to:
1. Respect the law.
2. Serve their communities.
3. Reject corruption.
4. Protect public property.
5. Promote peace.
6. Work hard.
7. Lead with integrity.
8. Solve local problems.
9. Value education and skills.
10. Contribute to national development.

7. PROGRAMME STRUCTURE
The programme runs for one full year and is divided into two phases.

7.1 Phase One: Seven Months of Classroom and Practical Training
Phase One is the foundation stage. It includes classroom teaching, spiritual formation, character training, leadership development, vocational training, entrepreneurship education and daily practical work.
During Phase One, trainees will participate in:
1. Morning devotion.
2. Bible study.
3. Leadership training.
4. Character formation.
5. Vocational classes.
6. Departmental practical work.
7. Entrepreneurship classes.
8. Community service.
9. Group discussions.
10. Assessments.
11. Mentorship sessions.
12. Evening reflection.
Phase One is a period of discipline. Trainees must develop the right attitude before they go for industrial attachment.

7.2 Phase Two: Five Months of Industrial Attachment
Phase Two is the workplace exposure stage. Trainees will be placed in approved organisations, workshops, farms, offices, businesses, ministries, agencies, companies or community service locations according to their department and training needs.
During industrial attachment, trainees must:
1. Report to their host organisation on time.
2. Obey workplace rules.
3. Respect supervisors.
4. Keep daily logs.
5. Submit reports.
6. Practise their skills.
7. Protect the name of the Academy.
8. Maintain Christian character.
9. Avoid misconduct.
10. Return for evaluations when required.
Proverbs 22:29“Seest thou a man diligent in his business? he shall stand before kings; he shall not stand before mean men.”
Industrial attachment is not a holiday. It is part of training.

10. SPIRITUAL LIFE EXPECTATIONS
The Academy is a Kingdom training environment. Spiritual formation is compulsory for every trainee.

10.1 Morning Devotion
Morning devotion is compulsory. It helps trainees begin the day with God, receive instruction, pray and align their hearts with the purpose of the day.
Trainees must:
1. Arrive on time.
2. Come with Bible and notebook.
3. Participate respectfully.
4. Avoid sleeping during devotion.
5. Listen carefully.
6. Apply the daily charge.
Psalm 5:3“My voice shalt thou hear in the morning, O LORD; in the morning will I direct my prayer unto thee, and will look up.”

10.2 Prayer
Prayer is central to the Academy. Trainees must learn to pray personally and corporately.
Trainees are expected to pray:
1. In the morning.
2. Before classes.
3. Before practical work.
4. During fellowship times.
5. In personal quiet time.
6. During evening reflection.
Luke 18:1“And he spake a parable unto them to this end, that men ought always to pray, and not to faint.”
Prayer builds spiritual strength, humility and dependence on God.

10.3 Bible Study
The Bible is the spiritual foundation of the Academy. Trainees must read, study and obey the Word of God.
Psalm 119:105“Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.”
Trainees must bring their Bible to approved spiritual activities and participate actively in Bible study.

10.4 Scripture Memorisation
Scripture memorisation helps trainees store God’s Word in their hearts.
Psalm 119:11“Thy word have I hid in mine heart, that I might not sin against thee.”
Trainees may be given weekly memory verses. Failure to participate without reasonable cause may affect character assessment.

10.5 Character Charge
A character charge is a short daily or weekly teaching that addresses behaviour, discipline, attitude, speech, morality, respect, diligence and responsibility.
Trainees must listen carefully and apply the charge.
Proverbs 4:23“Keep thy heart with all diligence; for out of it are the issues of life.”

10.6 Evening Reflection
Evening reflection helps trainees review the day before God.
Trainees should ask:
1. Did I obey instructions today?
2. Did I speak well today?
3. Did I work diligently today?
4. Did I respect authority today?
5. Did I offend anyone today?
6. Did I waste time today?
7. Did I learn something useful today?
8. What must I correct tomorrow?
Lamentations 3:40“Let us search and try our ways, and turn again to the LORD.”
