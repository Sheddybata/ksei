EBOMI KINGDOM LEADERSHIP ACADEMY
Full Weekly Timetable
1. Purpose of the Weekly Timetable
The weekly timetable of EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy is designed to train the whole person: spirit, soul, body, mind, character, skill and leadership capacity.
The timetable is structured to produce disciplined, prayerful, skilled, diligent, respectful, productive and service-minded trainees. Each day combines spiritual formation, physical discipline, classroom learning, technical training, practical work, mentoring, assignments, reflection and accountability.
The Academy does not only train trainees to know. It trains them to become. Therefore, every week must include:
Daily devotions.
Bible study.
Character charge.
Physical exercise.
Classroom lectures.
Technical training.
Departmental practical work.
Mentoring.
Assignments.
Evening reflection.
Weekly assessment.
Community service.

13. Closing Charge
The EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy weekly timetable is not only a schedule. It is a formation system. It trains the spirit through devotion, the body through exercise, the mind through lectures, the hands through practical work, the heart through character charge, the will through discipline and the life through service.
A trainee who follows this timetable faithfully will grow in discipline, knowledge, skill, character, leadership and service.
