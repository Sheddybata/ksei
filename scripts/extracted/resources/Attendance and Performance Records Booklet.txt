TITLE PAGE
EBOMI KINGDOM LEADERSHIP ACADEMY
Attendance and Performance Records Booklet
This booklet is prepared to help EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy keep accurate, clear and useful records for all trainees.
It is designed for paper-based record keeping and can also be transferred easily into Excel, Google Sheets or any digital database.
This booklet should be used by class monitors, trainers, mentors, departmental supervisors, administrative officers, monitoring and evaluation officers, industrial attachment coordinators and Academy management.
Records must be kept honestly, accurately, neatly and consistently.

1. PURPOSE OF ATTENDANCE AND PERFORMANCE RECORDS
The purpose of this booklet is to help the Academy track the progress, discipline, participation, conduct and readiness of every trainee throughout the one-year programme.
The records will help the Academy to know:
1. Who attends training regularly.
2. Who comes late repeatedly.
3. Who participates in morning devotion.
4. Who attends Bible study and spiritual formation sessions.
5. Who attends classroom lectures.
6. Who attends practical training.
7. Who attends departmental work.
8. Who attends mentoring sessions.
9. Who participates in evening reflection.
10. Who participates in community service.
11. Who completes industrial attachment.
12. Who performs well academically.
13. Who performs well practically.
14. Who shows good character.
15. Who needs correction, support or mentoring.
16. Who is ready for graduation.
17. Who should receive certification.
18. Who requires further training before certification.
Attendance is not only about being present. It shows discipline, responsibility, seriousness and respect for the opportunity given.

2. WHY RECORDS MATTER
Records matter because they protect truth, fairness and accountability.
Without good records, the Academy may not know who is truly faithful, who is improving, who is struggling, who is absent, who is late, who has completed assignments, who is ready for industrial attachment and who is qualified for graduation.
Good records help the Academy to:
1. Make fair decisions.
2. Support weak trainees.
3. Correct indiscipline early.
4. Reward diligence.
5. Track spiritual participation.
6. Track academic growth.
7. Track practical skills.
8. Track character development.
9. Track community service.
10. Track industrial attachment.
11. Prepare reports for management.
12. Prepare reports for sponsors, partners or parents where approved.
13. Maintain transparency.
14. Protect the name of the Academy.
Poor records create confusion, favouritism, arguments and injustice.

3. BIBLICAL FOUNDATION FOR FAITHFULNESS, ORDER AND ACCOUNTABILITY
3.1 Faithfulness
1 Corinthians 4:2“Moreover it is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful.”
Every trainee, trainer and record keeper must be faithful. Attendance, performance and conduct records must show the truth.

3.2 Order
1 Corinthians 14:40“Let all things be done decently and in order.”
The Academy must keep orderly records. Disorder in record keeping can damage fairness.

3.3 Diligence
Proverbs 22:29“Seest thou a man diligent in his business? he shall stand before kings; he shall not stand before mean men.”
Diligent trainees must be identified and encouraged. Repeated absence and lateness must also be recorded and corrected.

3.4 Accountability
Romans 14:12“So then every one of us shall give account of himself to God.”
Every trainee must give account of attendance, conduct, assignments, practical work and industrial attachment.

3.5 Truthfulness
Proverbs 12:22“Lying lips are abomination to the LORD: but they that deal truly are his delight.”
False records, fake attendance, false signatures and dishonest performance reports are unacceptable.

3.6 Careful Recording
Habakkuk 2:2“And the LORD answered me, and said, Write the vision, and make it plain upon tables, that he may run that readeth it.”
Written records help the Academy see progress clearly and act wisely.

4. GENERAL RECORD-KEEPING RULES
All record keepers must follow these rules.
1. Write clearly.
2. Use ink where possible.
3. Avoid unnecessary cancellation.
4. Do not tear pages from official registers.
5. Do not falsify attendance.
6. Do not sign for another trainee.
7. Do not change records secretly.
8. Correct mistakes neatly and initial the correction.
9. Record attendance at the actual time of the activity.
10. Do not fill attendance in advance.
11. Do not fill attendance after several days from memory.
12. Keep records in a safe place.
13. Submit records to the appropriate officer when required.
14. Protect confidential information.
15. Use the same attendance codes consistently.
16. Report repeated absence or lateness early.
17. Record approved absence properly.
18. Keep evidence for industrial attachment attendance.
19. Record performance fairly.
20. Record character observations based on evidence, not personal dislike.

5. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
5.1 Class Monitor
The class monitor assists the trainer and administrative officer in daily attendance tracking.
Responsibilities
1. Help distribute attendance sheets where approved.
2. Remind trainees to arrive on time.
3. Report absentees to the trainer.
4. Report late arrivals to the trainer.
5. Help keep the class orderly before the trainer arrives.
6. Never falsify attendance.
7. Never sign for another trainee.
8. Submit attendance sheet to the trainer or admin officer.
Class Monitor Warning
The class monitor does not have authority to excuse absence or change attendance records.

5.2 Trainer
The trainer is responsible for recording and confirming attendance and performance during classroom teaching.
Responsibilities
1. Take classroom attendance.
2. Confirm late arrivals.
3. Record participation.
4. Record academic performance.
5. Mark assignments fairly.
6. Report repeated lateness.
7. Report repeated absence.
8. Record discipline concerns.
9. Submit weekly academic records.
10. Keep records safe.
5.3 Mentor
The mentor tracks mentoring attendance, spiritual growth, character progress and personal support needs.
Responsibilities
1. Keep mentoring attendance.
2. Record mentoring topics.
3. Note agreed action points.
4. Report serious concerns through approved channels.
5. Track character improvement.
6. Encourage trainees who are struggling.
7. Protect confidentiality.
8. Submit mentoring summary where required.
5.4 Departmental Supervisor
The departmental supervisor tracks practical training, departmental work, safety conduct and skill development.
Responsibilities
1. Take departmental practical attendance.
2. Record punctuality during practical work.
3. Track tool use and safety conduct.
4. Assess practical skills.
5. Record practical assignments.
6. Track portfolio evidence.
7. Report unsafe behaviour.
8. Submit weekly departmental records.
9. Recommend trainees for additional practice.
10. Prepare practical clearance for graduation.
5.5 Admin Officer
The admin officer keeps central attendance and administrative records.
Responsibilities
1. Collect attendance records.
2. File daily, weekly and monthly records.
3. Track approved absence.
4. Prepare attendance summaries.
5. Keep warning forms.
6. Maintain individual student files.
7. Ensure records are properly stored.
8. Support digital transfer into Excel or Google Sheets.
9. Report missing records.
10. Support graduation clearance.
5.6 M&E Officer
M&E means Monitoring and Evaluation. The M&E officer reviews records to track progress, performance and programme quality.
Responsibilities
1. Review attendance trends.
2. Review performance trends.
3. Identify trainees at risk.
4. Track department progress.
5. Prepare monitoring reports.
6. Review industrial attachment records.
7. Support data accuracy.
8. Support monthly evaluation meetings.
9. Recommend improvement actions.
10. Provide evidence for graduation decisions.
5.7 Academy Management
Academy management uses records to make fair decisions.
Responsibilities
1. Approve record-keeping systems.
2. Review attendance and performance summaries.
3. Support trainees needing intervention.
4. Approve disciplinary actions.
5. Approve graduation clearance.
6. Protect confidential records.
7. Ensure all departments submit records.
8. Use records for improvement, not victimisation.
9. Ensure fairness in certification.
10. Maintain Academy standards.

38. RECORD STORAGE AND CONFIDENTIALITY RULES
Attendance and performance records must be protected.
38.1 Paper Record Storage
1. Keep records in labelled files.
2. Store files in a safe cabinet.
3. Do not leave registers in open places.
4. Protect records from rain, fire, termites and careless handling.
5. Do not allow unauthorised people to view records.
6. Keep confidential reports separately.
7. File records by month, class and department.
8. Keep warning forms in individual student files.
9. Keep industrial attachment records safely.
10. Submit final records to the admin office.
11. 2 Digital Record Storage
12. Use clear file names.
13. Save records in approved folders.
14. Back up important records.
15. Use passwords where possible.
16. Do not share files carelessly.
17. Do not alter digital records without approval.
18. Keep scanned forms where possible.
19. Restrict editing access.
20. Protect student data.
21. Report lost or corrupted files.
22. 3 Confidentiality Rules
23. Do not gossip with student records.
24. Do not expose character reports publicly.
25. Do not share medical or welfare information carelessly.
26. Do not use records to mock trainees.
27. Do not send records to outsiders without management approval.
28. Do not photograph record books casually.
29. Do not post attendance or discipline records online.
30. Keep complaints and mentoring notes private.
31. Share information only with authorised officers.
32. Always protect the dignity of trainees.
Proverbs 11:13“A talebearer revealeth secrets: but he that is of a faithful spirit concealeth the matter.”
A faithful record keeper protects confidential information.

41. FINAL NOTE ON HONESTY, ACCURACY AND ACCOUNTABILITY
Records are not ordinary papers. They are evidence of faithfulness, discipline, growth and readiness.
Every class monitor, trainer, mentor, supervisor, admin officer, M&E officer and manager must handle records with the fear of God.
Do not write false attendance.Do not protect indiscipline with false records.Do not punish innocent trainees with false reports.Do not change marks because of favouritism.Do not hide repeated absence.Do not ignore poor conduct.Do not exaggerate weakness.Do not use records to disgrace trainees.Do not destroy evidence.Do not treat record keeping as a small matter.
The Scripture says:
Luke 16:10“He that is faithful in that which is least is faithful also in much.”
A person who cannot be trusted with records cannot be trusted with greater responsibility.
Let every record keeper be honest.Let every trainer be fair.Let every trainee be accountable.Let every department be orderly.Let every decision be based on truth.Let EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy be known for discipline, excellence, integrity and faithful stewardship.
Final Scripture
Psalm 90:17“And let the beauty of the LORD our God be upon us: and establish thou the work of our hands upon us; yea, the work of our hands establish thou it.”
