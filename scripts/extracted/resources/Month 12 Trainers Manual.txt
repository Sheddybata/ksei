EBOMI KINGDOM LEADERSHIP ACADEMY
365-Day Trainer’s Manual
Month 12
Monthly Theme: Final Evaluation, Graduation Preparation and Deployment
MONTH 12 OVERVIEW
Purpose of Month 12
Month 12 is the final month of the one-year training journey. It focuses on final attachment evaluation, completion of reports, portfolio submission, graduation preparation, certification readiness, deployment planning and recommitment to Kingdom service.
Trainees have passed through orientation, character formation, leadership development, technical foundation, entrepreneurship, project management, workplace readiness and industrial attachment. Month 12 helps them gather evidence, receive final feedback, reflect on transformation, prepare for graduation and plan their next step after the Academy.
This month must not be treated casually. Many people start well but do not finish well. EBOMI trainees must finish with honour, discipline, gratitude, humility and readiness for service.
Main Outcomes for Month 12
By the end of Month 12, trainees should be able to:
1. Complete final industrial attachment reports.
2. Submit complete logbooks and weekly reports.
3. Update and submit portfolios.
4. Receive final supervisor evaluation.
5. Complete final Academy evaluation.
6. Prepare for graduation and certification.
7. Present evidence of learning and transformation.
8. Prepare employability and enterprise plans.
9. Recommit to Kingdom character and service.
10. Understand deployment into work, enterprise, further training or community service.
11. Honour God, trainers, mentors and host organisations.
12. Finish the programme with discipline and gratitude.

DAY 309
Topic: Understanding Final Attachment Completion
1. Learning Objectives
By the end of this lesson, trainees should be able to:
1. Understand what it means to complete industrial attachment.
2. Identify all required completion documents.
3. Prepare for final supervisor review.
4. Recognise the importance of finishing well.
5. Commit to honesty in all final submissions.
2. Scripture
2 Timothy 4:7
3. Memory Verse
Ecclesiastes 7:8
4. Teaching Notes
Final attachment completion means that a trainee has fulfilled the required workplace learning period and is now preparing to submit evidence of learning. It is not enough to say, “I attended attachment.” The trainee must show evidence.
Required completion evidence may include:
1. Daily logbook.
2. Weekly reports.
3. Monthly evaluation forms.
4. Supervisor feedback.
5. Attendance record.
6. Skill progress sheets.
7. Final attachment report.
8. Portfolio evidence.
9. Enterprise reflection.
10. Character reflection.
11. Mentor comments.
12. Final self-evaluation.
13. Finishing well means completing all reports truthfully, thanking supervisors, returning workplace property and leaving a good testimony.
5. Practical Activity
Each trainee should prepare a final attachment completion checklist and mark what is complete, pending or missing.
6. Group Discussion
Discuss:
Why is finishing well as important as starting well?
7. Case Study
A trainee worked well during attachment but delayed report submission. His final assessment suffered because his documents were incomplete.
8. Assessment
Assess through completion checklist and mentor review.
9. Homework
List all missing attachment documents and prepare a plan to complete them.
10. Leadership Application
Leaders do not abandon assignments at the final stage. They finish properly.
11. Character Development Goal
Character Focus: Completion Discipline
Trainees should complete all final requirements with truthfulness and order.
