TITLE PAGE
EBOMI KINGDOM LEADERSHIP ACADEMY
Alumni Follow-Up Programme
This programme is prepared to guide EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy in following up graduates after completion of the one-year training programme.
It is designed to help the Academy track graduate progress, support continued growth, measure long-term impact and maintain a strong alumni network of disciplined, skilled, godly and productive young people.
The Alumni Follow-Up Programme may be implemented through phone calls, WhatsApp messages, physical visits, Google Forms, Excel sheets, paper forms, alumni meetings, mentoring groups and community service projects.
Every graduate of EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy remains an ambassador of the Academy.

1. INTRODUCTION
EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy does not end its responsibility at graduation. Graduation is not the end of the journey. It is the beginning of deployment.
The Academy trains young people for spiritual maturity, character, vocational competence, leadership, entrepreneurship, community service and industrial attachment. After graduation, the Academy must know whether the training is producing lasting fruit.
Are graduates employed?Are they self-employed?Are they starting businesses?Are they continuing education?Are they serving in ministry?Are they serving communities?Are they demonstrating Kingdom character?Are they using the skills they learnt?Are they mentoring others?Are they contributing to nation building?
The Alumni Follow-Up Programme helps the Academy answer these questions with evidence.
John 15:16“Ye have not chosen me, but I have chosen you, and ordained you, that ye should go and bring forth fruit.”
Graduates must not only finish training. They must bear fruit.

2. PURPOSE OF ALUMNI FOLLOW-UP
The purpose of alumni follow-up is to track, support, encourage and measure the long-term growth and impact of graduates.
The programme helps the Academy to:
1. Know what graduates are doing after completion.
2. Track employment outcomes.
3. Track self-employment and business start-ups.
4. Track apprenticeships and further training.
5. Track further education.
6. Track ministry involvement.
7. Track community service.
8. Track skills utilisation.
9. Track Kingdom character and conduct.
10. Identify graduates who need support.
11. Identify graduates who can mentor others.
12. Identify graduates who can become future trainers.
13. Collect testimonies and impact stories.
14. Improve future training programmes.
15. Build a strong alumni network.
16. Encourage graduates to continue serving.
17. Measure the Academy’s contribution to community and nation building.

29. DATA PROTECTION AND CONFIDENTIALITY
Alumni data must be handled with care.
29.1 Data Protection Rules
1. Collect only useful information.
2. Inform alumni why information is being collected.
3. Keep forms safely.
4. Password-protect digital files where possible.
5. Do not share alumni phone numbers carelessly.
6. Do not publish testimonies without consent.
7. Do not release private financial information.
8. Do not expose personal struggles publicly.
9. Limit access to authorised officers.
10. Correct inaccurate records when discovered.
11. Back up important records.
12. Do not use alumni data for politics, manipulation or exploitation.
29.2 Confidentiality Rule
Personal challenges, welfare issues, financial struggles, family matters, conduct concerns and counselling details must be treated confidentially.
Proverbs 11:13“A talebearer revealeth secrets: but he that is of a faithful spirit concealeth the matter.”

33. FINAL CHARGE TO GRADUATES
Dear graduate,
Your graduation is not the end. It is the beginning of your assignment.
The Academy has trained you, but life will test you.The Academy has disciplined you, but you must now discipline yourself.The Academy has mentored you, but you must now continue to seek wisdom.The Academy has taught you to serve, but you must now serve without being forced.The Academy has given you skills, but you must now use them productively.The Academy has taught you integrity, but you must now walk uprightly when no one is watching.
Do not disappear after graduation.Do not abandon the values you learnt.Do not allow laziness to return.Do not allow bad company to corrupt you.Do not waste your skill.Do not despise small beginnings.Do not forget your community.Do not forget God.
Stay connected.Keep growing.Keep serving.Keep learning.Keep building.Keep praying.Keep working.Keep shining.
Matthew 5:14“Ye are the light of the world. A city that is set on an hill cannot be hid.”
You are light. Do not become darkness.You are trained. Do not become careless.You are skilled. Do not become idle.You are called to serve. Do not become proud.You are sent to lead. Do not forget humility.
Final Scripture
Psalm 1:3“And he shall be like a tree planted by the rivers of water, that bringeth forth his fruit in his season.”
May every EBOMI alumnus be fruitful in season, faithful in service, excellent in work, humble in leadership and useful in the hand of God for community transformation and nation building.
