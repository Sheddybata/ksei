EBOMI KINGDOM LEADERSHIP ACADEMY
365-Day Trainer’s Manual
Month 8
Monthly Theme: Industrial Attachment Orientation and Workplace Integration
MONTH 8 OVERVIEW
Purpose of Month 8
Month 8 begins the industrial attachment phase. Trainees have completed seven months of classroom, character, leadership, technical, entrepreneurial and workplace preparation. They are now entering real workplaces where their training will be tested and strengthened.
This month focuses on helping trainees settle properly into their host organisations, understand workplace culture, build respectful relationships with supervisors, observe carefully, keep accurate records, practise workplace discipline and begin supervised tasks with humility.
The main goal is workplace integration. Trainees must not rush, argue, complain or behave proudly. They must enter their attachment placements as learners, servants and representatives of the Academy.
Main Outcomes for Month 8
By the end of Month 8, trainees should be able to:
1. Report properly to their host organisation.
2. Understand workplace rules and expectations.
3. Build respectful relationships with supervisors and colleagues.
4. Observe workplace operations carefully.
5. Keep daily logs and weekly reports.
6. Follow safety rules.
7. Practise punctuality and professional conduct.
8. Accept correction with humility.
9. Begin supervised tasks responsibly.
10. Represent the Academy with honour.
11. Identify skills they need to develop further.
12. Complete the first monthly attachment review.

DAY 197
Topic: Reporting to the Host Organisation
1. Learning Objectives
By the end of this lesson, trainees should be able to:
1. Report properly to their host organisation.
2. Introduce themselves professionally.
3. Present required documents.
4. Understand where and to whom they should report.
5. Begin attachment with humility and order.
2. Scripture
Proverbs 22:1
3. Memory Verse
Colossians 3:23
4. Teaching Notes
The first day of industrial attachment is very important. It sets the tone for the trainee’s relationship with the host organisation.
A trainee should arrive early, dress neatly, greet respectfully and present himself or herself with humility. The trainee should carry the required documents and report to the right office or supervisor.
A proper introduction may be:
“Good morning, sir/ma. My name is __________. I am a trainee from EBOMI Kingdom Leadership Academy. I have been posted here for industrial attachment. I am grateful for the opportunity to learn and serve.”
Trainees should not behave as if the workplace owes them anything. They are there to learn. They should be polite, patient and attentive.
5. Practical Activity
Each trainee should practise a professional introduction before reporting or during Academy follow-up. The trainee should also prepare a checklist of documents submitted to the host organisation.
6. Group Discussion
Discuss:
Why is the first impression important during industrial attachment?
7. Case Study
A trainee arrived early, greeted respectfully and submitted all documents. The supervisor immediately noticed his seriousness. Another trainee arrived late and without documents, which created doubt about his discipline.
8. Assessment
Assess through supervisor confirmation, trainee report and review of submitted documents.
9. Homework
Write a short reflection on:
How I reported to my host organisation and what I learnt on my first day.
10. Leadership Application
Leaders begin assignments with order, respect and preparation.
11. Character Development Goal
Character Focus: Order and Respect
Trainees should report properly, speak respectfully and behave with humility.
DAY 198
Topic: Understanding the Workplace Induction
1. Learning Objectives
By the end of this lesson, trainees should be able to:
1. Explain workplace induction.
2. Identify important information given during induction.
3. Understand workplace rules.
4. Ask respectful questions.
5. Record key induction lessons.
2. Scripture
Proverbs 19:20
3. Memory Verse
James 1:19
4. Teaching Notes
Induction is the introduction given to new workers or trainees in a workplace. It may include rules, safety guidelines, reporting structure, working hours, dress code, supervisor details, prohibited conduct and expected behaviour.
During induction, trainees should listen carefully and take notes. They should not assume they already know everything.
Important things to learn during induction include:
1. Reporting time.
2. Closing time.
3. Department or unit assignment.
4. Supervisor’s name.
5. Safety rules.
6. Dress code.
7. Tools or equipment rules.
8. Confidentiality expectations.
9. Attendance process.
10. Break times.
11. Emergency procedures.
12. How to ask for help.
5. Practical Activity
Each trainee should complete an induction note sheet:
1. My supervisor is:
2. My unit or department is:
3. My reporting time is:
4. Three workplace rules I learnt are:
5. Two safety rules I must obey are:
6. One question I need to ask is:
6. Group Discussion
Discuss:
Why should trainees take workplace induction seriously?
7. Case Study
A trainee ignored the induction briefing and later broke a safety rule because he did not listen. The supervisor had to correct him publicly.
8. Assessment
Assess through induction note sheet and supervisor feedback.
9. Homework
Write one page on:
The most important things I learnt from workplace induction.
10. Leadership Application
Leaders listen carefully when entering a new environment.
11. Character Development Goal
Character Focus: Attentiveness
Trainees should listen carefully and take notes during induction.
